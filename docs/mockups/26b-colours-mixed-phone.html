Colours that stand out

One look made from A, B and C, with three choices for the four kinds of assets.

  • From A: white cards with dark, readable text, and gains and losses on a light chip of their own colour.
  • From B: the net worth on a deep green card in white, the change in mint.
  • From C: each kind of asset its own colour, the same in the split bar, its tile and Investments.
  • Light mode: clear, saturated colours on white, so each part of the bar pops. Dark mode: brighter versions on near-black, with near-white text, so everything reads in high contrast.
