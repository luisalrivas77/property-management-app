  • 1What the first figure contains.
  • 2Where the second figure comes from.
  1. Same rows as the panel. Label on the left, figure on the right, the figure in bold, aligned like every other list in the side panel.
  2. The sign gets its own column. + − × ÷ sit in a narrow column on the left, so the math reads top to bottom like a receipt. The first row has no sign.
  3. The result is a row too. A heavier line above it, “=” in the sign column, a light green background, and the same figure as the panel’s main figure.
  4. Several steps stack. Each step gets a small heading; a result that carries into the next step starts that step with ↳.
  5. Words go in the legend. Below the math, smaller and grey. A small number on a row points to its legend line. A line with no number is about the whole calculation.
  6. No sentence with numbers in it. If a figure is in the math, it is not repeated in the legend.
