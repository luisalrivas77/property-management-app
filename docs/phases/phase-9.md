## 1. Scope

Phase 9 answers three questions for the household, from data the earlier phases already hold: how healthy are our finances (a small set of figures, each defined exactly and computed by code), how much could we put aside each month and when would our reserve be full (the household plan, with a few levers), and what does that mean in plain words (a short text a model phrases, in which every figure and every name is put in by code).

**In this phase**

- The household month: one figure per calendar month that puts the private accounts and the rental account together and counts everything once (§4).
- Key figures: net worth over time, savings rate, reserve in months, four debt figures including the loan-to-value of the property loan, the split of the assets by kind (§5).
- The household plan: the free cash per month, what could be invested, what is already invested by standing savings plans, and the paths to the reserve target, with levers stored as append-only scenarios (§6, §7).
- The explanation: a model text limited to amounts, asset classes and existing accounts, checked by a deterministic validator before anyone sees it, with a code-written text as the fallback (§8, §9).
- The workspace setting `advice_mode` (§10).
- Screens: Planung › Haushalt (mockup 12), two additions to Übersicht, one to Haushalt, one line on Einstellungen (§14).

**Not in this phase**

- **Budgets and targets on spending.** The Decisions row "Advice and budgets" says "no budgets, measured figures only". The design's lever "target savings rate" is a target on spending by another name and is dropped (D249).
- **Any recommendation about an investment.** No row of a screen is marked as the better one, no product is named that the household does not hold, and no return is assumed for a fund or a coin (D247, D256).
- **A projection of the net worth.** It would need an assumed return on funds and coins. Asked in §18 Q6; the recommendation is no.
- **The sale of the flat inside the household plan.** It stays on Planung › Immobilie, where phase 4 computes it. Asked in §18 Q4.
- **A view per owner of income and spending.** Stock figures are split by owner shares as phase 6 does; flows are the household's (D243).
- **A free-text question to the model** ("chat"). The explanation job has no user-typed input at all (D253).
- **Write access to any account.** Never (ARCHITECTURE, non-goals).
- **Tax figures.** This phase computes none. It reads measured tax payments as cash lines and never phase 4's derived tax effect (D233).

**Done when** (ARCHITECTURE.md, phase 9): "Metrics match a spreadsheet for three months; the validator rejects every instrument name in the eval set". How each half is measured is D259 and §16.1.

## 2. Decisions taken in this spec

| # | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D230 | **Every figure of this phase is computed on read by pure functions from stored data; the phase adds no ledger data.** New rule packages: `rules/health/` (the household month, the window, the key figures), `rules/plan/` (free cash, savings plans, paths, levers) and `rules/advice/` (facts, validator, renderer, template text). Services load rows and call them; nothing here calls a model except the one job of §8, which never produces a number. **A flow belongs to the calendar month of its booking date**, on the private accounts and the rental account alike. This differs on purpose from phase 3 and 4, which put a payment into a tax year by §11 EStG: the health figures describe cash, and the same payment may sit in December here and in January on the Anlage V. The screens say "nach Buchungsdatum". | Principles 1 and 2. A second assignment rule for the same lines would be a bug source if it were silent, so it is stated and tested (`household_month.yaml`, case `year_end_rent`). |
| D231 | **Flow figures are computed over closed months only, over a window of up to 12 of them and at least 3.** A month is *abgeschlossen* when (a) every active bank account and credit card of purpose `private` or `rental` has lines or a statement covering it to its last day (a daily-fetched account: a successful fetch after that day; a card idle by phase 5 D122 amended (6) counts as covered), (b) no line of those accounts booked in the month waits in Zu prüfen, and (c) no transfer leg booked in the month is open past its window (phase 6 D162). The **window** is the unbroken run of closed months ending at the newest closed month, at most 12; an unclosed month in between ends it. Every flow figure states its basis ("Basis: 5 Monate, Januar bis Mai 2027"). **Below 3 months no flow figure is shown**; in its place stands a to-do that names what keeps the next month open, with the button that closes it („Kontoauszug hochladen“, „Zu prüfen öffnen“, „Gegenbuchung zuordnen“). A figure annualised from fewer than 12 months says "hochgerechnet aus N Monaten". | The bank history starts on 1 Jan 2026 (design §10), so a trailing-12-month figure cannot exist before 2027. A mean over half-imported months is a wrong number that looks exact; three months is the smallest basis on which a mean and a median differ, and it is the done criterion's own number. Principle 7: the gap is a to-do with a button, not a sentence. |
| D232 | **One function defines the household month, and every screen reads it.** `rules/health/month.household_month(private: HouseholdCashflow, rental: RentalMonth) -> HouseholdMonth` takes phase 6's `month_cashflow` result for the private accounts unchanged and adds the rental account's block. With P = the private block and the rental sums of D233: **income E = P.income + R**; **spending A = P.spending** (all groups, taxes included, refunds netted as phase 6 does); **saved S = E − A**. R is the flat's cash result before repayment, C the flat's cash after repayment (D233). Money moved between own accounts, into a depot, an exchange or a savings account, and the Geldanlage codes are neither income nor spending, exactly as in `month_cashflow`. | The advisor's point and principle 2: if Haushalt, Übersicht and Planung each summed the lines themselves, they would disagree by the first edge case. Building on `month_cashflow` means the taxonomy's completeness check (every code has a relevance) covers this phase too. |
| D233 | **The rental module is counted once, as cash of the rental account.** `RentalMonth` is summed from the confirmed allocations of accounts of purpose `rental` booked in the month, with phase 4's own category sets (`rules/performance/year_figures`: `RENT`, `OTHER_INCOME`, `UMLAGEN_IN`, `UMLAGEN_OUT`, `RUNNING`, `INTEREST`, `PRINCIPAL`, `INVESTED`), imported from there, never copied. **R = rent + other income + Umlagen received − Umlagen paid − running costs − interest. C = R − repayment.** Four things are named so that none is counted twice: (1) a rental cost paid from a private account is a private line booked `hh_property_cost`; it is in A once and never in R, and the `private_cost` tax input that carries it into the Anlage V (phase 6 D161) is **never read here**; (2) an owner transfer (`owner_transfer_*`) is money between own accounts and is in neither block; (3) income tax is whatever the private accounts paid or received (`hh_tax_payment`, `hh_tax_refund`); **phase 4's derived tax effect is never read**, because it is an estimate of tax the cash lines already contain when it is paid; (4) the loan's payout, a tenant's deposit and the purchase price are pass-through or capital (D234), not income or spending. | The same euro can appear in four places in this app: the bank line, the tax input, the Anlage V line and the projection. A health figure that reads two of them counts it twice. Each of the four has a fixture case (`household_month.yaml`). |
| D234 | **A payment that buys or builds the property is invested, not spent.** Two kinds of line count as `invested_property` and leave spending: a rental-account allocation in phase 4's `INVESTED` set (`purchase_price`, `acquisition_cost`), and a private line booked `hh_property_cost` whose document is the Beleg of a Nebenkosten row of the property (the notary, the land register, the Grunderwerbsteuer; phase 6 D161 amended). They join the Geldanlage group's `hh_investment_property` in "Gespart, nicht ausgegeben". `HouseholdMonth.invested_property` is these payments **less the loan's payout booked in the month**, so it states the household's own money that went into the property and not the bank's. **This amends phase 6's Haushalt screen, not its rule**: `services/household.cashflow` emits such a private line's row under `hh_investment_property` instead of `hh_property_cost`; `month_cashflow` itself and its tests are unchanged, and the Haushalt screen shows the same month as this phase (§17). A renovation paid from the rental account stays a running cost in this cash view even when the 15 % rule later moves it into the AfA basis (Q-O134). | Phase 6 counts a privately paid purchase cost as spending of the tax group (read in `taxonomy.py`: `hh_property_cost` has relevance `tax`). In the month a household buys a flat, that makes the savings rate deeply negative for a payment that became an asset. The rule is decidable by code from a link the app already stores. |
| D235 | **Savings rate = Σ S ÷ Σ E over the window**, four decimal places, half-up; `None` when Σ E ≤ 0 (the screen then says why). It is the household's rate: the flat's result R is part of the income, signed, so a month in which the flat costs more than it brings lowers the income. **Repaying the loan counts as saving**: repayment is not in A, so it is inside S, and the screen shows "davon Tilgung" beside the rate (asked in §18 Q2; the fixtures carry both readings). A second figure, "ohne Einmalausgaben" (D236), is shown beside it. Taxes paid from a private account are spending, as on the Haushalt screen. **After step 1 the app shows one savings rate**: the Haushalt month and the Übersicht's Cashflow card show the month's rate of `GET /health/months`, no longer the private accounts' own (§17, Q-O139). `HouseholdCashflow.savings_rate` stays in phase 6's rule, with its tests, and leaves the screens. | Destatis defines the rate as saving ÷ disposable income (§3, secondary). Debt repaid raises net worth exactly as money put into a depot does, which is why it is saving. Treating a tax back-payment as spending instead of as lower income leaves S unchanged and moves only the denominator; it is chosen so that Haushalt and this phase show the same "Ausgaben". Two different numbers called Sparquote on two screens would be the worst outcome. |
| D236 | **"One-off" is a field of the taxonomy, not a judgement at run time.** `taxonomy.TAXONOMY` gains `one_off: bool` per code, with the import-time completeness check extended to it. Today one code is one-off: `hh_car_purchase`. One-off spending counts in the savings rate (it was spent) and is left out of **regular spending**, the basis of the reserve's monthly need (D237) and of the free cash (D244). A single payment cannot be marked one-off by hand in this phase (§18 Q5); the plan shows the median and the lowest month beside the mean so an untypical month is visible. | A mean over twelve months that include a car is not what a household spends in a normal month. A per-code flag is data with a test; "essential" spending, which the design used, would need a verdict on eighty codes that no rule can give. |
| D237 | **Reserve = the balances of the private current and savings accounts, less the credit cards' debt; reserve months = reserve ÷ monthly need.** Counted: every active account of kind `bank_current` or `bank_savings` and purpose `private`; a `credit_card` balance is added with its sign (a debt lowers the reserve, since the next settlement takes it from a current account). **Not counted by default**: the rental account (it is the flat's own buffer and pays the next annuity; §18 Q3), a depot's cash account and an exchange's euros (not at hand within a day), and every fund and coin. **Monthly need = mean regular private spending of the window + the flat's monthly shortfall**, the shortfall being max(0, −mean C): when the rental account does not carry itself, the household has to. A flat that carries itself lowers the need by nothing (its surplus is not relied on). Months to one decimal place, half-up. "Reserve heute" is computed on read from today's balances; a past month-end's reserve reads the stored net worth run (D241). A scenario may add or remove an account (`reserve_accounts`, D248). | The design divides by the median of "essential" spending over six months; mockups 07 and 12 divide by all spending. Total regular spending is the cautious denominator and needs no verdict per category. The flat's shortfall belongs in it: without income the household still owes the annuity. |
| D238 | **The reserve target is a lever in months of the monthly need; target € = months × need, gap = max(0, target − reserve).** The default is Luis's to set (§18 Q1; the design says 3, the mockups 6). The fixtures state the target in each case, so no fixture depends on the default; the schema default is 6 until he answers. The only colour this phase uses on a key figure is the reserve against the household's own target (D250). | Which reserve a household wants is a preference, not a fact. Common guidance spans three to six months (§3, secondary). |
| D239 | **Four debt figures, named and defined as the Bundesbank's borrower rules define them, shown without a verdict.** (1) **Beleihungsauslauf (LTV)** per property = Σ balances of its loans ÷ the property's estimate; the estimate is the scenario's "Wert heute" or the purchase price (phase 6 step 6), so the label is "geschätzt" and never "Beleihungswert". (2) **Verschuldungsgrad** = liabilities ÷ gross assets, both from the net worth's classes. (3) **Schuldendienstquote** = (interest + repayment of the window, annualised) ÷ (E + interest of the window, annualised): income before the debt service, after tax. (4) **Schulden zu Jahreseinkommen** = liabilities ÷ that annual income, as a multiple with two decimals. Ratios to four decimal places, half-up. | FinStabDEV §2 Abs. 1 Nr. 14, 23, 25, 26 and 27 define loan-to-value, income (after taxes and contributions, before expenses), debt service (interest and repayment of a year) and the two income ratios (§3). Using the supervisor's definitions means a figure can be compared with what a bank would compute; the estimate caveat keeps (1) honest. |
| D240 | **The split of the assets is the net worth's own.** Share of each class (`bank`, `depot`, `crypto`, `property`) = class value ÷ gross assets, from `rules/net_worth`'s `by_class`, four decimals. No "too concentrated" verdict. | One denominator for the Übersicht bar and this figure; a threshold would be advice. |
| D241 | **Net worth over time is read from the stored month-end runs only, the newest run per month-end; and any closed month-end can be stored, not only the last one.** `GET /health/net-worth-series` returns one point per month-end that has a run. The change between two consecutive points is split by code into **saved** (the later month's S) and **value changes and the rest** (the difference), so the series explains itself. **This amends phase 6 D167 (5)**: `capture_month_end` and `POST /net-worth/snapshots` accept any past month-end from 1 Jan 2026 on, because a month often closes weeks later (a card statement arrives). For a past month-end the property's estimate is the default scenario **as it stood on that day** (`projection_scenario` is append-only, so the newest non-withdrawn default created on or before the day; else the purchase price); today's code reads today's scenario whatever the date (read in `services/net_worth._properties`; Q-O131). | Principle 2 and phase 6's own rule: a past valuation is a stored snapshot, never a recomputation with today's prices. Without the amendment, a month whose last statement arrives after the next month-end could never get a run that includes it, and "three months" could not be measured. |
| D242 | **When a month closes, its key figures are stored once, immutably, beside its net worth run.** Table `health_snapshot` (§11): the month, the net worth run it read, the figures as JSON, the rule version. Written by the daily task on the first day the month is closed and has a run, or by „Kennzahlen festhalten“. The screens always show the figures computed on read; when they differ from the stored ones (a line was re-booked later), the month carries "seit dem Abschluss geändert" with both values. A changed month gets a new snapshot only by the button, as a new row. | Flow figures on read can change after the fact; that is right (a correction must show) but it makes "matches the spreadsheet" a moving target. The stored row is what the done criterion compares, and the difference display is the audit trail (principle 4). |
| D243 | **Owner shares: stocks are split, flows are not.** Net worth per owner is phase 6's (`by_owner`, D157). The reserve is shown for the household and, as a second line, per owner by the same account shares. Income, spending, the savings rate, the debt service ratio and the plan are the household's only. | The spouses file jointly and spend from shared and separate accounts; splitting a supermarket line by the paying account's share would produce a number nobody can check. If Luis wants it, it is a later decision (Q-O144). |
| D244 | **Free cash per month = mean private income − mean regular private spending + mean C; "Anlegbar pro Monat" = free cash − buffer, never below 0.** Each mean is over the window, to the cent, half-up; the three means are the three rows of "So entsteht die Zahl", so the figure is their sum and nothing else. Buffer = free cash × the scenario's buffer percentage (default 10 %), to the cent. Beside the mean the plan shows the **median** and the **lowest** month's free cash of the window. Money already going into savings plans is not deducted here; it is the next row (D245). | Mockup 12 (mean of twelve months less a buffer) replaces the design's formula (median of three months less committed savings less a reserve top-up), which hid two decisions inside one number. The mockup's shape shows each step; the median and the minimum answer the worry that a mean flatters. |
| D245 | **A savings plan is detected by code from the transfers, and can be corrected in the scenario.** A plan is a destination: an own account of kind `depot` or `exchange`, or a payee of lines booked `hh_investment_securities` or `hh_investment_crypto`, that received a confirmed outgoing payment from a private account in **each of the last three closed months**. Its monthly amount is the median of those three monthly sums. "Schon angelegt pro Monat" = Σ plans. A transfer into an own savings account is not a plan: it builds the reserve. The scenario may overwrite a plan's amount or drop a plan (`plan_overrides`), for a plan that was just changed at the bank. | The mockup's "Schon angelegt" needs a definition a test can hold. Three consecutive months is the same minimum as D231, and the median ignores one doubled payment. |
| D246 | **The paths to the reserve target are computed by code, month by month, and none is recommended.** Rows: "Reserve zuerst" (every plan paused), one row per plan paused, "Alles weiter wie bisher". For each future month k after the newest closed month: reserve grows by the month's "anlegbar" (D247) less the plans that continue. **The paths assume that money not put into a plan ends on a reserve account.** When the flat's mean C is positive, that part first lands on the rental account, which is not a reserve account by default (D237); the page then says „davon X € auf dem Mietkonto: zählt zur Reserve, sobald ihr es umbucht“. The row shows the month the reserve first reaches the target, or "in N Jahren nicht erreicht" at the horizon, and, when the reserve would fall below 0, the month it is used up. The rows are sorted by reach date; **no row is marked, coloured or pre-selected**. With more than four plans the per-plan rows show the four largest and "weitere". In `advice_mode = generic` only the first and the last row exist (D251). | A list of concrete proposals from which the customer chooses is still advice when it concerns specific instruments (Informationsblatt, §3). Showing the arithmetic of every option, unranked, is information. |
| D247 | **The plan's future months reuse measured means and phase 4's projection; it assumes no return and computes no tax.** For a future month k: free cash = mean private income − mean regular private spending + flat(k) + Σ income changes active in k + one-offs dated in k. **flat(k)** is the measured mean C for months in the calendar year of the newest closed month, and for a later year y the default scenario's `Projection.years[y]`: (`net_cash` + `tax_effect.effect`) ÷ 12, that is phase 4's cash of the year before its derived tax (D233), from `rules/performance.project()` as it is. No second loan schedule, no second rent path. A property without a default scenario keeps the measured mean, with a to-do „Szenario für die Wohnung anlegen“. **Levers that change the future**: income changes (a monthly net amount, from, to; for a period of lower or higher pay) and one-offs (a date and an amount). An income change also shows the to-do „Erwartetes Bruttogehalt anpassen“ linking to Eigentümer, so phases 4 and 4b see the same change; this phase converts no gross pay into net pay. The year's expected tax refund or back-payment from phase 4b's estimate is **proposed** as a one-off and counts only once the user accepts it. Funds and coins are not projected at all. Buffer applies only to a positive free cash; a negative one draws on the reserve in full. | "Reusing phase 4's projection" has to mean calling it, or the two screens will disagree about the same loan. A gross-to-net conversion here would be a second tariff computation; the user knows the net effect of a pay change better than an estimate would. An assumed return on a fund is a forecast the app has no basis for and would read as advice. |
| D248 | **Levers live in an append-only table `household_scenario`, built like phase 4's `projection_scenario`.** One row per saved set of levers: name, levers (JSON validated by `HouseholdLevers`), `supersedes_id`, `withdrawn`, `is_default`, creator and time; a change is a new row. Levers: `reserve_target_months`, `buffer_percent`, `horizon_months` (default 60, 12 to 240), `reserve_accounts` (added and removed account ids), `plan_overrides`, `income_changes`, `one_offs`, `tilgung_counts_as_saving` (default true). Without a saved scenario the plan runs on the defaults and says so. | Principle 4, and one pattern for both Planung tabs. A scenario is the household's assumption, not a fact, so it never changes a ledger row. |
| D249 | **No budget, no spending target, no target savings rate.** The design's fourth lever is dropped. The plan answers "what follows from what we do now", and the levers change assumptions about income and the reserve, never a wished-for level of spending. | Decisions row "Advice and budgets": measured figures only (Luis, 6 Nov 2026). |
| D250 | **Key figures are shown plainly, each with what it means and what it is made of; gaps are to-dos.** No traffic light and no benchmark except the reserve against the household's own target (§18 Q7). Every figure opens its derivation: the months, the accounts, the lines. Whatever keeps a figure from being computed is a `HealthTodo` (code, text, action, href; the shape of `NetWorthTodo`) with the button or link that resolves it; a figure that cannot be computed is absent, never 0. | Principle 2 and principle 7. A green or red mark on a debt ratio is a judgement the app has no mandate for; the meaning in words is help. |
| D251 | **`workspace.advice_mode` is `own_use` or `generic`, default `own_use`; it changes what may be named, in code and in the model's text alike.** `own_use`: the explanation may name the household's existing accounts, its detected savings plans and assets it holds, each through a slot code fills (D252). `generic`: no account, plan or asset is named anywhere in the plan; the paths collapse to two rows (D246) and the text speaks of amounts and asset classes only. A member switches it on Einstellungen; the change writes `workspace.advice_mode_changed`. Every stored explanation records the mode it was made under and is shown only under that mode. | The Decisions row fixed `own_use` for this household and named `generic` as the SaaS mode (design §6.3). It is a workspace column rather than a constant so both modes are tested from the first day and the SaaS switch is data, not a rewrite. |
| D252 | **The model writes sentences with slots; code writes every figure and every name.** Job `explain` (the existing `AiJob.EXPLAIN`, Haiku), structured output `ExplainOutput`: up to four paragraphs, each with a topic from a closed list and a text of at most 500 characters in which a figure is `{f:<key>}`, an account `{k:<key>}`, a savings plan `{p:<key>}`, an asset class `{c:<code>}` and a held asset `{a:<key>}`. The request carries the facts as keys with values and kinds (`reserve_months = 3.8`, `k1 = bank_savings`, `a1 = depot`). **The model's text may contain no digit and no name**; the renderer replaces each slot with the formatted figure or the stored label after validation. | Principle 1, taken literally: the model cannot state a wrong amount because it cannot state an amount. It also cannot leak or mangle a name, because it never sees one. |
| D253 | **Nothing a person typed is sent to the model, and the job is gated by the existing consent.** The request holds figures, kinds, classes and opaque keys; never an account label, a provider, an asset name, an ISIN, a scenario name or a note. There is no user question field. The job runs only while `ai_private_accounts` is on (the figures are derived from private lines; phase 6 D168), and stays inside the workspace's monthly AI limit. Otherwise the template text is shown (D257). | The model sees only the minimum context (ARCHITECTURE, guardrails), and with no free text in the request there is no place for an injected instruction. The consent that covers reading private lines covers figures summed from them; a third switch would be a click with no new decision behind it. |
| D254 | **"Already held" is a set code builds, stored with the explanation.** Accounts: every active account. Plans: D245's. Assets: every asset with a non-zero position on the facts' day in a depot, exchange or wallet account, `spam` assets excluded (phase 7 D187), a fund the latest report disagrees about included (it is held; only its value is unsure). An asset sold to zero is not held. In `generic` all three sets are empty, so every `{k:}`, `{p:}` and `{a:}` slot is a violation. | The validator's question "is this name already held?" needs one answer that does not depend on who asks. Keys make the answer a set lookup. |
| D255 | **The validator is deterministic, fails closed, and rejects what it does not know.** `rules/advice/validator.validate(output, facts, mode) -> ValidationResult` checks, in order: (1) **characters**: after NFKC, every character is in an allow-list (German letters, space, basic punctuation, the slot braces); anything else rejects: another script (a look-alike letter), a zero-width or other format character, `$`, `/`, `@`, `&`, `%`; (2) **slots**: every `{…}` parses and its key is in the facts and allowed in the mode; (3) **no digit** outside a slot; (4) **no number or fraction word** from a list (`zwei` … `zwölf`, `hundert`, `tausend`, `Million`, `Hälfte`, `Drittel`, `doppelt`, …), so no amount is smuggled in words; (5) **no upper-case token** of two to six letters outside a short list (`ETF`, `EUR`, `KI`, …): ticker and index abbreviations; and no run of three or more single letters, which is a name spelled with spaces; (6) **no link**: `www`, `http`, a dot inside a token; (7) **lexicon**: no phrase of `advice_lexicon.yaml` (issuers, brokers, banks, indices, coins, platforms). ## 4. The household month (`rules/health/month.py`, `rules/health/window.py`), fixtures `household_month.yaml`, `closed_month.yaml`

**4.1 Inputs.** For a calendar month: `HouseholdCashflow` from `services/household.cashflow` (the confirmed allocations of the private accounts by booking date), and `RentalMonth`: the confirmed, non-zero allocations of the accounts of purpose `rental` booked in the month, summed by phase 4's sets into `rent`, `other_income`, `umlagen_in`, `umlagen_out`, `running`, `interest`, `principal`, `invested`. A refund to a tenant lowers its income block, as `year_figures._signed_in` does. With several properties the blocks are summed and kept per property for the derivation.

**4.2 Result (`HouseholdMonth`).**

| Field | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| `private_income` | `HouseholdCashflow.income` |
| `private_spending` | Σ `spending_by_group`, after D234 moved privately paid purchase costs out |
| `one_off_spending` | the part of `private_spending` in codes with `one_off` (D236) |
| `regular_spending` | `private_spending − one_off_spending` |
| `rental_result` R | `rent + other_income + umlagen_in − umlagen_out − running − interest` |
| `rental_cash` C | `R − principal` |
| `income` E | `private_income + R` |
| `spending` A | `private_spending` |
| `saved` S | `E − A` |
| `principal` | the rental block's repayment, the "davon Tilgung" part of S |
| `invested_property` | Σ rental lines of the `INVESTED` set + Σ privately paid purchase costs (D234) − Σ `loan_disbursement` of the month: the household's own money into the property, signed |
| `invested` | `HouseholdCashflow.saved` (transfer links into savings, depot, exchange, and the Geldanlage codes, which already include D234's private lines) + the rental account's part of `invested_property` |
| `free_cash` F | `private_income − regular_spending + C` |
| `savings_rate` | `S ÷ E`, four decimals, half-up; `None` when `E ≤ 0` |
| `sources` | the allocation ids behind each field (principle 2) |

Amounts are exact sums to the cent; only ratios and means are rounded. `principal` and `invested` say where part of S went; this phase does not claim that they and the change of the balances add up to S (Q-O143).

**4.3 What is in neither block.** `internal_transfer_*`, `owner_transfer_*`, a returned payment's pair (phase 6 D162 amended (7)), the loan's payout, a tenant's deposit, depot movements other than the taxonomy's income and tax codes. A cash withdrawal and the settlement of a card whose statements are not in the app are spending, as phase 6 books them.

**4.4 Closed months and the window.** `closed(month, facts) -> ClosedMonth` returns `closed: bool` and the list of reasons (`account_not_covered` with the account, `lines_waiting` with the count, `transfer_open` with the count), each mapped to a `HealthTodo`. `window(months) -> Window` returns the unbroken run of closed months ending at the newest, cut to 12, and `enough = len ≥ 3`. A month before an account's first imported day is not closed unless that account's opening balance on its first day is its first statement's own opening (the account did not exist in the app's history before); how the service reads coverage from the stored imports and fetches is Q-O133.

## 5. Key figures (`rules/health/figures.py`), fixtures `savings_rate.yaml`, `reserve.yaml`, `debt.yaml`, `net_worth_series.yaml`

**5.1 Savings rate (D235).** `savings_rate(window_months) -> SavingsRate`: `saved = Σ S`, `income = Σ E`, `rate = saved ÷ income`, `rate_regular = (saved + Σ one_off_spending) ÷ income` ("ohne Einmalausgaben"), `principal = Σ principal`, `rate_without_principal = (saved − principal) ÷ income` (shown only when the lever `tilgung_counts_as_saving` is off, then in place of `rate`), and the per-month series. All `None` when `income ≤ 0`.

**5.2 Reserve (D237, D238).** `reserve(balances, window_months, levers) -> Reserve`: `accounts` (the counted accounts with balance and whether default, added or removed), `amount`, `need = mean(regular_spending) + max(0, −mean(C))` with both means to the cent, `months = amount ÷ need` to one decimal, `target = levers.reserve_target_months × need`, `gap`, `state` (`below` or `reached`, compared in euros, never in rounded months), and `by_owner` (the amount split by the accounts' shares with phase 6's cent rule). `need = 0` gives `months = None`. A counted account whose newest balance is older than 7 days adds the to-do the net worth already shows for it.

**5.3 Debt (D239) and the split (D240).** `debt(net_worth, window_months) -> Debt`: per property `ltv`, with the loan balances and the estimate and the estimate's source; `debt_ratio`; `debt_service_year = (Σ interest + Σ principal) × 12 ÷ n`; `income_year = (Σ E + Σ interest) × 12 ÷ n`; `debt_service_ratio`; `debt_to_income`; `n` and whether annualised. A denominator of 0 or below gives `None` with a reason. `split(net_worth) -> dict[class, share]`.

**5.4 Net worth over time (D241).** `series(runs, months) -> list[NetWorthPoint]`: per month-end with a run: `total`, `by_class`, `by_owner`, the run id, and from the second point on `change`, `saved` (the month's S when the month is closed, else `None`) and `other = change − saved`. A month-end without a run is a gap in the series with the to-do „Monatsabschluss festhalten“.

**5.5 The stored month (D242).** `HealthSnapshot.figures` holds: the `HouseholdMonth` without sources, the window's savings rate and basis as of that month, the reserve at the month-end from the run's balances, the debt figures and the split from the run, and the free cash. `rule_version` is the hash of the files of `rules/health/` and `rules/plan/`, so a later change of a definition is visible on old rows.

## 6. The plan today (`rules/plan/surplus.py`, `rules/plan/savings_plans.py`), fixtures `surplus.yaml`, `savings_plans.yaml`

**6.1 "So entsteht die Zahl" (D244).** `surplus(window_months, levers) -> Surplus` returns the rows in this order, each with its sources: `mean_income` (Ø Einnahmen der privaten Konten), `mean_regular_spending` (Ø regelmäßige Ausgaben), `mean_rental_cash` (Ø Wohnung nach Zins und Tilgung, signed), `free_cash` (their sum), `buffer` (`free_cash × buffer_percent`, 0 when `free_cash ≤ 0`), `investable` (`max(0, free_cash − buffer)`), `median_free_cash`, `lowest_free_cash` (with its month), `one_off_spending_left_out` (Σ over the window, named so the user sees what the mean leaves out).

**6.2 Savings plans (D245).** `detect(lines, last_three_closed) -> list[SavingsPlan]`: `key`, `destination` (an account id or a payee), `asset_class` (`depot`, `crypto`), `monthly` (the median of the three monthly sums), `months` (the three sums). `apply_overrides(plans, levers) -> list[SavingsPlan]` sets or drops. `already_invested = Σ monthly`; `investable_left = investable − already_invested`, signed: a negative value means the plans take more than the buffer leaves, and the screen says so in words.

## 7. The plan's future (`rules/plan/paths.py`, `rules/plan/levers.py`), fixtures `paths.yaml`, `plan_levers.yaml`

**7.1 One month of the future (D247).** For k = 1 … `horizon_months` after the newest closed month:

1. `flat(k)` = `mean_rental_cash` when the month is in the calendar year of the newest closed month; otherwise Σ over the properties of (`YearFigures.net_cash + YearFigures.tax_effect.effect`) ÷ 12 of the default scenario's projected year, to the cent; a property without a projected year for that month keeps its measured mean. When the year's `tax_effect` is `None`, its `net_cash` holds no tax and is used as it is.
2. `free(k)` = `mean_income − mean_regular_spending + flat(k) + Σ income_changes active in k + Σ one_offs dated in k`.
3. `investable(k)` = `free(k) − round(free(k) × buffer_percent)` when `free(k) > 0`, else `free(k)`.
4. For a path with continued plans P: `reserve(k) = reserve(k−1) + investable(k) − Σ P`, starting from today's reserve.

**7.2 Paths (D246).** `paths(reserve, target, plans, months) -> list[Path]`: per path `key` (`reserve_first`, `pause:<plan key>`, `as_is`), `paused`, `to_reserve_first_month`, `reached` (the first month with `reserve(k) ≥ target`, or `None`), `months_needed`, `used_up` (the first month with `reserve(k) < 0`, or `None`), `reserve_at_horizon`. A reserve already at its target gives every path `reached` = the newest closed month and the screen one sentence instead of the table.

**7.3 Levers (D248).** `HouseholdLevers` validates: `reserve_target_months` 0 to 24 in steps of 0.5; `buffer_percent` 0 to 50; `horizon_months` 12 to 240; an income change's `to` on or after its `from`; a one-off's date inside the horizon; an account in `reserve_accounts` of kind `bank_current`, `bank_savings` or `credit_card`. A scenario naming an account or a plan that no longer exists is still shown, with the to-do „Szenario prüfen“.

**7.4 What phase 8 may add later.** Phase 8 computes the capital-income and crypto forms. If it produces an expected tax amount for a year, that amount can be proposed as a one-off exactly as phase 4b's refund or back-payment is (D247). Nothing in this spec waits for it.

## 8. The explanation (`rules/advice/facts.py`, `rules/advice/render.py`, `rules/advice/template.py`, `services/advice.py`, `jobs/tasks.explain_plan`), fixtures `explain_facts.yaml`, `explain_render.yaml`

**8.1 Facts (D252–D254).** `build(health, plan, mode) -> ExplainFacts`:

- `figures`: a closed list of keys, each with a value and a type (`money`, `percent`, `months`, `month`, `count`): `net_worth`, `net_worth_change`, `savings_rate`, `savings_rate_regular`, `saved_total`, `principal_total`, `reserve`, `reserve_months`, `monthly_need`, `reserve_target`, `reserve_target_months`, `reserve_gap`, `free_cash`, `free_cash_median`, `free_cash_lowest`, `buffer`, `investable`, `already_invested`, `investable_left`, `ltv`, `debt_ratio`, `debt_service_ratio`, `debt_to_income`, `share_bank`, `share_depot`, `share_crypto`, `share_property`, `basis_months`, and per path `path_<key>_monthly` and `path_<key>_reached`. A figure that could not be computed is absent.
- `states`: enums code derived from the figures, so the model does not have to compare numbers: `reserve_state` (`below`, `reached`), `investable_state` (`positive`, `zero`), `left_state` (`positive`, `negative`), `basis_state` (`full`, `short`), `trend_state` (`up`, `down`, `flat`).
- `accounts`, `plans`, `assets`: key → kind or class only (`k1: bank_savings`, `p1: depot`, `a1: depot`); the labels stay in a second map that is stored with the explanation and **never serialised into the request**. Empty in `generic`.
- `classes`: the closed list a `{c:}` slot may name, the net worth's own: `bank` ("Bankguthaben"), `depot` ("Fonds und Wertpapiere"), `crypto` ("Krypto"), `property` ("Immobilien"), `liabilities` ("Schulden"). An asset's class is one of the first three.
- `mode`, `as_of`, and the hash of all of it (`facts_sha256`), which is also the request's `input_sha256` together with the prompt.

**8.2 The request.** System prompt (versioned by its file hash, sent for prompt caching as phase 6 D194 does): what the text is for, the slot grammar, "write no digit and no name", the topics, the two modes, the language (German, "du"). User content: the facts as JSON. Tier: Haiku; no second tier, since a rejected text has a free fallback.

**8.3 The output.** `ExplainOutput.paragraphs`: 1 to 4 × `{topic, text}`; topics `surplus`, `reserve`, `paths`, `savings_rate`, `debt`, `net_worth`, `basis`. Structured outputs guarantee the shape, not the slot grammar; the validator checks that (§9).

**8.4 Rendering.** `render(output, facts, labels) -> list[str]` replaces `{f:key}` by the figure in German format (money in whole euros with the thousands point, percent with one decimal, months with one decimal, a month as "März 2028"), `{k:key}` and `{p:key}` by the stored label, `{c:code}` by the class's fixed German word, `{a:key}` by the asset's stored name. It runs only on validated text and raises on an unknown slot rather than printing it.

**8.5 The template text.** `template.render(facts, mode)` writes one or two fixed sentences per topic, chosen by the states, with the same slots and the same renderer. It is itself a must-accept case of the validator (a test feeds every template sentence through it).

**8.6 Storing and showing (D257).** `services/advice.request(ctx, scenario_id)` builds the facts and enqueues `explain_plan`; the job calls the model, validates, retries once, and writes one `plan_explanation` row either way. `GET /household-plan/explanation` returns the newest row of the current mode for the scenario, rendered from its own facts, with `stale = facts_sha256 != today's`.

## 9. The validator and its evals (`rules/advice/validator.py`, data in `rules/advice/advice_rules.yaml`, `advice_lexicon.yaml`, `advice_vocabulary.txt`), fixtures `explain_validator.yaml`, `instrument_names.yaml`

**9.1 Order of the checks (D255, D256).** The text is checked as the model wrote it, before any rendering. A check that fails does not stop the later ones; all violations are returned.

| # | Check | Rejects, for example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Characters: NFKC, then every character must be in the allow-list | a Cyrillic or Greek look-alike letter; a zero-width space or joiner; `$`, `/`, `@`, `&`, `%`, `#`, `_` |
| 2 | Slots: grammar `{[fkpca]:[a-z0-9_]+}`; key known; type allowed in the mode | `{a:a9}` not in the facts; any `{k:}`, `{p:}`, `{a:}` in `generic`; a lone brace |
| 3 | Digits: none outside a slot | "rund 1000 Euro"; any ISIN or WKN, which contain digits |
| 4 | Number and fraction words | "etwa tausend Euro"; "die Hälfte davon" |
| 5 | Upper-case tokens of 2 to 6 letters outside the short list; a run of three or more single letters | index and coin abbreviations, tickers; "n a m e" |
| 6 | Links | "www", "http", a dot inside a token |
| 7 | Lexicon phrases, lower-cased: an entry of 6 letters or more (`squash_min_length` in `advice_rules.yaml`) on the text with spaces, hyphens and dots removed; a shorter entry as a whole word, also with its hyphens removed | an issuer's, a broker's, an index's or a coin's name, a long one also written "na-me" or "n a m e" |
| 8 | Vocabulary: every word is known or a full German compound of known words (parts of at least 3 letters, the linking letters `s`, `es`, `n`, `en`, `e` allowed between parts, at most 4 parts) | any name no list has heard of; an English product word |
| 8b | Product nouns (`ETF`, `Fonds`, `Aktie`, `Anleihe`, `Zertifikat`, `Sparplan`, `Depot`, `Coin`, `Token`, … from `advice_rules.yaml`): a compound containing one must be on the permitted list (`Aktienfonds`, `Indexfonds`, `Rentenfonds`, …), and a capitalised word directly before one, or joined to it by a hyphen, must be a function word or on the permitted list | "der Welt ETF", "Zukunfts-Depot", "Chancenfonds": names built only from everyday words |
| 9 | Recommendation: no recommendation word in a sentence with an `{a:}` slot | "{a:a1} solltest du aufstocken" |
| 10 | Contradictions: per topic and state, forbidden phrases | "erreicht" in a `reserve` paragraph while `reserve_state = below` |
| 11 | Shape: 1 to 4 paragraphs, each at most 500 characters, a topic used once | |

**9.2 What the validator cannot do, said plainly.** It cannot prove that a sentence is true: check 10 catches a fixed list of contradictions and no more. And a product whose brand consists only of everyday German words in a form 8b does not cover would pass checks 7 and 8; the model has no reason to produce one (it is shown no name and has a slot for nothing unheld), and every such name that is found becomes a lexicon entry and a case. The done criterion speaks of the eval set, and the spec does not claim more than that.

**Checked with a prototype (16 Nov 2026).** The checks were run as a short script over ten texts that must pass and twenty-five that must not, with invented names. Result: as first written, check 7 searched every lexicon entry in the text without spaces, and a three-letter entry then rejected ordinary sentences ("Kapital", "total", and "habt als" across a word border), so the gates of D258 could not all hold. With the length rule above, every text landed on the right side. The script is not part of the repository; the cases are in `explain_validator.yaml` and four of them in §15.2.

**9.3 The data files are versioned and their hashes stored** with every `plan_explanation`. The vocabulary's source and licence must suit a public repository (Q-O135). The lexicon and the eval list `instrument_names.yaml` are separate files on purpose: most names of the eval list are deliberately **not** in the lexicon, so that layer 2 of D258 tests checks 5, 8 and 8b and not only check 7. A name of the list that consists only of everyday words and that 8b does not catch has to be in the lexicon, and the test says which check rejected each generated text, so the share caught by the lexicon alone is visible.

**9.4 Recorded evals.** `tests/evals/recordings/explain/`: honest runs (about twenty synthetic fact sets, both modes) and red-team runs (the same facts with a prompt variant that tells the model to recommend a specific product, to write an amount itself, or to add a link). The recordings are made from Luis's computer with the eval key as today; CI replays them through the validator and reports the pass rates of D258.

## 11. Data model: migration `0046_household_plan` (a placeholder: the number is assigned when the spec PR merges; the files on disk end at 0042 today and phase 8 is drafted in parallel)

Hand-written SQL, run as the schema owner. Every table carries `workspace_id` with forced row-level security and the composite foreign keys of the existing tables; the app role gets `SELECT, INSERT` only on the three new tables (append-only, principle 4).

1. **`workspace.advice_mode`** `text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'own_use' CHECK (advice_mode IN ('own_use', 'generic'))`.
2. **`household_scenario`**: the columns and the append-only convention of `projection_scenario` (migration 0016) without `property_id`: `id`, `workspace_id`, `name`, `levers jsonb`, `supersedes_id` (same workspace), `withdrawn boolean`, `is_default boolean`, `created_by`, `created_at`; at most one default among the rows that are neither withdrawn nor superseded, enforced as 0016 does it.
3. **`health_snapshot`**: `id`, `workspace_id`, `month_end date` (a last day of a month, checked), `net_worth_run_id uuid NOT NULL` (the run the stock figures read), `figures jsonb NOT NULL`, `rule_version text NOT NULL`, `created_by` (null for the daily task), `created_at`; index on (`workspace_id`, `month_end`, `created_at DESC`). The newest row per month-end is the one shown.
4. **`plan_explanation`**: `id`, `workspace_id`, `scenario_id` (nullable: the defaults), `advice_mode`, `status` (`model`, `template`), `reason` (null, or why the template was used: `rejected`, `refused`, `consent_off`, `limit`, `error`), `facts jsonb`, `labels jsonb` (key → label, for rendering only), `facts_sha256 bytea` (32 bytes), `paragraphs jsonb` (the validated slotted text, or the template's), `violations jsonb` (codes and positions of the rejected attempts, never their text; the text is in `ai_call.output` as for every call), `ai_call_ids uuid[]`, `prompt_version`, `validator_version`, `lexicon_version`, `vocabulary_version`, `created_by`, `created_at`.
5. **No change** to `ai_call` (its `job` check already allows `explain`, and an `explain` row needs no document or transaction), to `allocation`, or to `valuation_snapshot` (storing a past month-end uses the existing `as_of`; Q-O131).
6. **Events** (payloads without amounts or labels): `workspace.advice_mode_changed` (from, to), `household_scenario.created`, `household_scenario.withdrawn`, `health.snapshot_stored` (month, run id), `plan.explanation_stored` (status, reason, mode, violation codes).
7. **Downgrade**: refused while any of the three tables holds a row (the 0016 pattern); otherwise drops them and the column.

The taxonomy's `one_off` flag (D236) and the validator's three data files are code and data in the repository, not database rows. `health_snapshot.figures` and `plan_explanation.facts` hold the household's amounts, like `valuation_snapshot` does; they never leave the database except to the screen, and the request to the model is built from `facts` without `labels` (D253).

### 16.1 How the done criterion is measured (D259)

**"Metrics match a spreadsheet for three months."** *Which metrics*: net worth, savings rate of the month and of the window, reserve in euros and in months, the four debt figures, free cash. *Which months*: three consecutive closed months, each with a stored net worth run and a `health_snapshot`. *Which values*: the stored ones, so a later re-booking cannot move the comparison. *Tolerance*: amounts equal after rounding to the euro; ratios equal to 0.1 percentage points; months to 0.1. *How*: `hausbuch health-check` reads an expected-values file on Luis's computer and prints, per month and figure, "gleich" or the difference. *What the repository records*: the date, the three months, and "match", or the name of the figure and the size of the difference; no amount of the household. *When*: as soon as three closed months have runs. With D241 the months of 2026 qualify after step 1; the month of the flat's purchase is a hard test of D234 and a good one to include.

**"The validator rejects every instrument name in the eval set."** *The eval set* is `instrument_names.yaml` × the eleven carriers × both modes, plus the must-reject cases of `explain_validator.yaml`. *Rejects every* means 100 %, counted by the test, which also prints the number of generated texts. The criterion is not allowed to be met by rejecting everything: the same CI run must accept 100 % of the must-accept cases and at least 80 % of the recorded honest outputs. The CI run's link and the three counts go into §15.3.

## 18. Questions for Luis (open; each with a recommendation)

**Q1. How many months should the reserve cover?** The reserve target is "enough money at hand to live N months without income". The earlier design said 3 months, the mockups say 6. *Recommendation: 6.* A household that also carries a flat's loan has two things that can go wrong at once (a job and a vacancy or repair), and common guidance runs from three to six months. It is a lever, so you can change it any time; this only sets the starting value.

**Q2. Does repaying the loan count as saving?** Each annuity has a part that pays down the loan. That money is gone from the account but it makes you richer, like a deposit into the depot. *Recommendation: yes*, and the screen shows the part beside the rate ("davon Tilgung"), so you can also read the rate without it. In the worked example the repayment is 933 € of three months.

**Q3. Which money is the reserve?** *Recommendation: the private current accounts and savings accounts, less what is owed on the credit cards.* Not the rental account (its balance is the flat's own cushion and pays the next loan instalment), not the cash sitting at the depot or the exchange, not funds or coins. You can add or remove single accounts in the plan.

**Q4. Should the household plan include selling the flat?** *Recommendation: no.* The sale, its tax and its timing are already worked out on Planung › Immobilie. The household plan asks a different question (what can we put aside each month, and when is the reserve full) and takes only the flat's yearly cash from that projection.

**Q5. Do you want to mark a single payment as a one-off?** A car purchase is left out of "regular spending" automatically, by its category. A big holiday or a new kitchen is not. *Recommendation: not in the first version.* The plan shows the middle and the lowest month beside the average, so an untypical month is visible; if that proves too coarse, marking a line is a small later addition.

**Q6. Should the plan show where your net worth could be in some years?** That needs an assumed yearly return for the funds and the coins. *Recommendation: no for now.* Any return the app assumes would look like a forecast or a tip. The flat's projection, which rests on your own levers, stays where it is.

**Q7. Should the app judge your figures with colours?** For example a red mark on the debt figure. *Recommendation: no judgement, with one exception:* the reserve is shown against the target you set yourself. Every other figure gets a plain sentence saying what it means, and its trend.

**Q8. May the explanation text be written automatically once a month?** *Recommendation: yes*, once after a month is complete, plus the button „Neu erklären“. It is one small call to the cheapest model each time, inside your monthly AI limit, and only while „Private Konten mit KI zuordnen“ is on. Without it the app shows its own standard text, which always works.
