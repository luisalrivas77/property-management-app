Phase 6 turns the landlord ledger into the household's ledger. It adds no tax law: the forms for capital income and crypto (Anlage KAP, Anlage SO) are phase 8, the lots and holding periods phase 7, the health metrics phase 9. What it adds is **data and plumbing**: (1) every account of the household with its **kind**, its **purpose** (rental or private) and **owner shares**; (2) the household's private bank accounts, at up to three institutes, imported through the **unchanged** statement path (camt, CSV, PDF, FinTS, plus a reader for DKB's CSV export) with one FinTS connection per institute and login, and categorised by a **household taxonomy** that lives beside the property categories, with the rules the app learns per merchant; (3) a **transfer matcher** that links every movement between two own accounts so none is ever counted as income or cost; (4) the depot read from its **documents** (statements, quarterly reports, trade confirmations, distribution notes) into asset movements and holdings that reconcile against the report; (5) the exchange's **CSV export** into the same asset movements, with no model call; (6) **assets, prices and valuations** on an append-only price table fed by four free public sources behind one `PriceSource` (the ECB, CoinGecko, Kraken, and the ETF issuers' own daily NAV files, Luis's choice of 12 Nov 2026), so one month-end net worth is reproducible; (7) the **Konten**, **Anlagen** and **Haushalt** screens of mockups 08, 09, 09b and 10, and the net-worth cards of the Übersicht (mockup 07). The principle that governs every piece: a private account's line can touch a tax figure only by becoming a **confirmed input of the form that already reads it** (a privately paid rental cost of phase 3, a donation or §35a service of phase 4b), never by a second path into the draft.

## 1. Scope

**In**

- **Account kinds, purpose and owner shares** (D156, D157): `bank_current`, `bank_savings`, `depot`, `exchange` (and `wallet`, reserved for phase 7, refused at creation); `purpose = rental | private`; `account_ownership` rows that mirror `property_ownership` (numerator and denominator, Σ = 1, a reason text, so "joint 50/50 because the coins were paid from the joint account" is stored with the record, as agreed on 6 Nov 2026).
- **The household's private current accounts** (three, at three institutes: one joint account of A and B and one sole account per owner, each institute with its own FinTS server and login; no savings account; Luis, 12 Nov 2026, §17 Q1; which account is at which institute is in the app's records only, never here) imported through the **unchanged** path: a Sparkasse's camt ZIP export or CSV-CAMT export, DKB's CSV export through a reader of its own (D173), the bank's PDF statement, or the FinTS sync of phase 5 with **one connection per institute and login**, each login's PIN a named container secret (D163). The two banks the app supports from this phase are the Sparkassen (as phase 5 built it, any BLZ) and DKB (D173: decoupled approval in the DKB app, camt only if its BPD offers it, verified at the first real connection). The phase 2 guard "an owner account holds no statements" goes (D156). History from 1 Jan 2026 (agreed 6 Nov 2026).
- **A household taxonomy** beside the property categories (D159): income, spending groups as mockup 10 names them, transfers, and the few tax-relevant codes, each either outside every form (`TaxTreatment.PRIVATE`) or a **proposal into the input the form already reads** (D161). A **per-purpose matching order** (D158): the rent, loan and Hausgeld matchers never run on a private account. **Name-pattern rules** for card payments, which carry no IBAN (D160), so the second-month criterion rests on learned rules, not on the model.
- **The transfer matcher** (D162): one pure rule over every own account, bank or exchange or depot, with a `transfer_link` row per matched pair; the rental account keeps its `owner_transfer_*` categories and the matcher of phase 2; the private side gets `internal_transfer_*`; a leg without a counterpart is a review card once its window has passed.
- **The depot by documents** (D165): the cash statements (Verrechnungskonto and the portfolio's Kontoauszug) as PDF statements of the depot account through the existing PDF path, their lines categorised by booking text in code; the **trade confirmations** and **distribution notes** read into `asset_movement` rows through the one pre-filled form (phase 2 D132); the **quarterly report** read into `holding_statement` rows that every position reconciles against, with the report's values as prices. The **tax certificate** is classified, stored and kept; its figures are read in phase 8, when the form that needs them exists.
- **The exchange by CSV** (D166): the BISON transaction history imported into `asset_movement` rows with **no model call**, deduplicated on the exchange's own transaction id, its EUR legs linked to the bank by the transfer matcher, its crypto "Deposit" lines (bonuses: the export carries no external address) proposed as `cashback_reward` for review, its per-asset balance typed once as the declared closing.
- **Assets, prices, valuations, net worth** (D167, D174): an `asset` registry (ETF by ISIN, crypto by symbol and CoinGecko id, cash per currency); an append-only `price` table with a date-first source precedence; a `PriceSource` interface with the ECB daily reference rates, CoinGecko's daily history, Kraken's daily OHLC and **the ETF issuers' own daily NAV per ISIN** (`issuer_nav`, one adapter per issuer, the documents as the fallback for an issuer without a usable page; Luis, 12 Nov 2026, §17 Q4); valuations computed on read; an immutable `valuation_snapshot` per month-end; a net worth that includes the property's estimate from phase 4 and the loan balances.
- **Screens** (§13): Konten regrouped with kinds, owner chips and „Stand“; Anlagen with the Übersicht and ETF tabs (the Krypto tab is phase 7); Haushalt with Einnahmen, Ausgaben, Sparquote and the spending groups; the Übersicht's Nettovermögen and Vermögensaufteilung cards; the „Private Konten mit KI zuordnen“ switch on Einstellungen (D168).
- **Model visibility** (D168): private-account lines and the depot's documents reach the model only after a new workspace switch `ai_private_accounts` is on, with the consent of the member who switches it on (both owners agreed on 12 Nov 2026, §17 Q2; owner B's own click in the app was required until the same evening, D168 as amended), and with the same minimisation as the rental account's lines.

**Out**

- Wallets, chains, lots, FIFO, holding periods (phase 7); Anlage KAP and SO, the Sparer-Pauschbetrag card, the Vorabpauschale card and the Freistellungsauftrag record (phase 8, D170); net-worth-over-time, savings rate targets, reserve months, debt ratios (phase 9: the Haushalt screen of this phase shows measured figures only, no target).
- Budgets (declined 6 Nov 2026); any write access to an account; naming an investment product (non-goal).
- Historical crypto prices before the first movement (phase 7's lots need them; the Demo tier's 365-day limit is a phase 7 fact); historical ETF NAVs before the first confirmed movement of the asset (the issuers' files reach back years, but nothing earlier is stored, D174).
- An MT940 reader over FinTS: designed as a contingency in D173 and built only if DKB's bank parameters offer no camt; a third bank (ING, comdirect, …): nothing in the design excludes one, nothing is tested.
- A non-property loan: none exists (Luis, 12 Nov 2026, §17 Q6); the net worth subtracts the property loans only (D167), and a later consumer loan would need `loan.property_id` relaxed and a spec amendment.
- A lot, a holding period or an acquisition date for any asset (phase 7); this phase only stores what phase 7 needs to carry them across a transfer (D175).
- Reading the depot's tax certificate into figures, and the quirion "Steuerreport" (phase 8).
- Betriebskostenabrechnung, email-in changes, reminders for the new kinds (one reminder, "Quartalsbericht erwartet", is listed as cheap in Q-O63 and not built).
