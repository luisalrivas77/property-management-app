## 1. Scope

**In**

- Actuals per property and year from confirmed allocations and the phase 3 draft (§5.3).
- The marginal rate derived from each owner's expected income on the §32a splitting tariff, Soli and church tax, as per-year rule data; a Bescheid's zvE or a typed rate overriding it per year (§4, §5.1, §5.2).
- The projection to a chosen sale year that runs phase 3's `draft()` over synthesized payments, so every tax rule (AfA incl. D79 and the D80 kitchen line, §82b, the 15 % window, §11) applies without new code (§5.4).
- At sale: §23 check with the AfA add-back, profit formula, profit bridge, CAGR, IRR on dated cash flows (§5.5).
- Scenarios (levers) as append-only rows; projections computed on read (§6).
- The Planung screen, mockup `05-performance` (§9).

**Out**

- Betriebskostenabrechnung generator: proposed as an unscheduled optional step (§11, §13 Q1).
- Anlage N and the Hauptvordruck (a later phase may build them on this phase's tariff engine; §7 keeps the door open).
- Totalüberschussprognose for verbilligte Vermietung (phase 3 Q7 said phase 4 "may"; not in this spec: it is the same projection with a different horizon, a later amendment).
- Vorfälligkeitsentschädigung on an early loan repayment at sale (the selling-costs lever can carry an estimate).
- Kinderfreibeträge, Progressionsvorbehalt (Elterngeld), Kirchensteuer-Kappung, other income kinds in the zvE: not modelled (§5.1 names the error direction; the override covers them).

## 4. The tariff as data (`rules/tax/tariffs/2026.yaml`, `schemas/performance.py`)

class TariffZone(Schema):

## 5. Rules

Pure functions, no I/O, no model; `Decimal` throughout; errors carry a code (§10).

### 5.1 Tariff and tax effect (`rules/tax/tariff.py`)

- `income_tax(zve, tariff, joint) -> Decimal`: floor `zve` to whole euros; joint → `2 × tax(floor(zve ÷ 2))`; each zone per §32a; result floored to whole euros.
- `soli(est, tariff, joint) -> Decimal`: 0 when `est ≤ Freigrenze`; else `min(est × 5.5 %, (est − Freigrenze) × 11.9 %)`, floored to the cent.
- `church_tax(est, owners, tariff) -> Decimal`: each owner with `church_tax` pays their state's rate on half the joint ESt (`est ÷ 2 × rate`, half-up to the cent); an owner outside a church pays nothing; the two halves are summed, so one member at 8 % gives half the two-member figure and two members in different states each pay their own rate. This is exact when both spouses are members (Halbteilung); when only one is, the state laws apportion the joint ESt by the ratio of each spouse's Grundtabelle tax on their own Einkünfte (Art. 9 Abs. 2 BayKiStG), so the half is an approximation (about €21 a year on the tax effect for the §10.3 household; the besondere Kirchgeld of other states is not modelled either; review of 9 Nov 2026). No effect on Luis's data: neither spouse is a member (§13 Q2). Open question Q-O6.
- `total_tax(zve, tariff, owners) -> TaxBreakdown {est, soli, church, total}`.
- `tax_effect(zve_base, R, tariff, owners) -> TaxEffect {effect, effective_rate, breakdown_with, breakdown_without}` per D82; `slope(zve_base, …)` for R = 0, for display only: the §32a zone's derivative at `zve_base ÷ 2` × (1 + the Soli's marginal factor at that base, 0 below the Freigrenze + the owners' church rates ÷ 2), so it is comparable with the effective rate, which includes Soli and church tax (fixed by step 0: `effect_zero_shows_slope` 36.1 %, the bare §32a derivative being 33.4 %).

Worked examples (fixture `tariff.yaml`): single 2026: 12,348 → 0; 17,799 → 1,034; 17,800 → 1,035; 30,000 → 4,217; 69,878 → 18,213; 100,000 → 30,864; 277,826 → 105,551. Joint: 60,000 → 8,434; 90,000 → 17,670; 200,000 → 61,728. Soli joint on ESt 42,000 → 154.70 (Milderungszone), 48,000 → 868.70, 50,000 → 1,106.70; on 17,670 → 0.00. Church tax 8 % on 17,670 → 1,413.60. Effect (`marginal_rate.yaml`): base 90,000, two owners BY in church, R = −7,948.66 → tax 19,083.60 → 16,275.60, effect **−2,808.00**, effective rate 35.3 %; R = +2,787.00 → +1,013.04 (36.3 %); R = 0 → 0.00 with the slope shown.

### 5.4 Projection (`rules/performance/synthesize.py`, `projection.py`)

`project(facts: DraftFacts, scenario: Levers, owners, inputs, today, loans: list[LoanTerms]) -> Projection {years: list[YearFigures + value, loan_balance, equity], sale: SaleResult, assumptions[]}`.

Per projected year Y from `today.year + 1` to the sale year, with `n = Y − today.year`:

1. **Rent** = Σ rent rows in force on `today` (`rent_at`) × 12 × `(1 + rent_growth)^(n−1)` (the first projected year keeps today's rent), minus `vacancy_months ÷ 12`; `rent_cold` monthly allocations dated the tenancy's `rent_due_day`, `recurring`, service period the month; months a real recurring rent line has already paid (e.g. January's rent received on 29 December, which §11 puts in the next year) are not synthesized again (fixed by step 0; without it the §10.3 2027 checkpoint would be 11,050.00, not 12,360.00). Vacant months come off the end of the year. NK prepayment likewise into `operating_costs_prepayment` (a `flat_rate` or `prepayment` tenancy; `included` has none).
2. **Costs**, per recurring category (`hausgeld_umlagefaehig`, `hausgeld_verwaltung`, `hausgeld_ruecklage`, `grundsteuer`, `insurance`, `property_management`, `bank_fees`): the latest confirmed line before `today` is annualised as `amount × 12 ÷ cadence_months` and repeated at that cadence, where `cadence_months` = the months of the line's service period when set, else the gap in days between the category's two latest lines ÷ 30.4, rounded and snapped to {1, 2, 3, 6, 12}, else 12 (a lone line is taken as yearly). The fixture's Hausgeld parts (180/40/100 monthly), management (25.00) and bank fees (4.90) give 2,160 / 480 / 1,200, 300.00 and 58.80; its two Grundsteuer lines of 60.00 on 17 Aug and 16 Nov (91 days → quarterly) give 240.00. Each category then grows by `(1 + cost_inflation)^(n−1)`; **upkeep** = the lever × `(1 + cost_inflation)^(n−1)` (it grows like the other costs; scenario B's 2028 upkeep is 612.00), as one `repairs` line on 30 June with a synthetic `exclude_from_15pct` (reason `annual_maintenance`), so the 15 % tracker does not capitalise routine upkeep (D56's exclusion is exactly for it). Hausgeld Rücklage contributions stay `hausgeld_ruecklage` (tracked); no `ruecklage_spent` is assumed.
3. **Loan** from `build_schedule(terms)` with the scenario's follow-up rate override, else the loan's; each entry → `loan_interest` and `loan_principal` on `due_on`, recurring, period = the month; `loan_balance` = the year's closing balance.
4. **Draft** = `draft(facts + synthetic rows, Y, rule_set_or_latest(Y))` with `checklist = None`, `today = None`, `coverage = []`, `pending = 0`; warnings are dropped except `draft.form_provisional` → `assumptions`. `taxable_result`, `afa`, `afa_movable` from it.
5. **Tax effect** per §5.1 with `zve_base(Y)` (carried forward, D83).
6. **Value** = `today_value × (1 + value_growth)^n`; **equity** = value − loan balance.
7. `year_figures` over the synthetic rows.

Fixture inputs and checkpoints are in §10.3.

### 10.4 Conventions fixed by the step 0 fixtures (9 Nov 2026)

Step 0 filled every open row with an independent script (Decimal, the real phase 3 `draft()`); every figure this spec states came out unchanged. Where the spec was silent, the fixtures fix the convention, and steps 1–2 follow them:

- A loan entry's service period is the month of its due date.
- Growth applies to a category's yearly total, rounded half-up once a year, then spread in equal cents over its rows, the last row taking the rest.
- An override effect is `R × rate`, rounded half-up; the slope is shown only when R = 0, the effective rate only otherwise.
- Per-owner zvE breakdowns are listed only under an estimate (empty under a Bescheid); `tax_estimated` is set whenever the effect's source is not a Bescheid.
- A sale gain is split per owner half-up, the last owner taking the remainder; the Freigrenze test is `0 < share < 1,000`, so a loss is never "below" it.
- CAGR and IRR are rounded half-up to six decimals; tests allow ±0.000001 against D89's 1e-6 bisection.
- Sale blocks only in the three scenarios and C's control (`useful_life_control_sale_2037`); the upkeep, rent-growth and vacancy cases assert their years only.
- `soli_full_50000` keeps its name although 50,000 of ESt is still inside the Milderungszone (the full 5.5 % starts at about 75,677); the file adds an 80,000 check.
- `tax_state` is text with a CHECK of the 16 codes; the 0016 carry-over of `owner.marginal_tax_rate` writes no event (the old value stays in the owner events).
- Pflegeversicherung 3.6 % is cited from PBAV 2025 §1 Abs. 1 (BGBl. 2024 I Nr. 446), closing Q-O3.
- **Net cash subtracts the tax effect (corrected 9 Nov 2026, from the step 2 review).** The spec, the fixtures and the fixture loader had `net_cash = … + tax_effect`, carried over from ARCHITECTURE's "+ tax effect", which was written when the tax effect meant `−result × rate` (positive for a loss). D82 made it `T(zvE + R) − T(zvE)`, negative for a loss, without flipping the cash formula, so every net cash showed the tax saving as a cost: about 3,000 € a year too negative for a household like §10.3's, twice the cumulative saving in the profit. The step 0 check reproduced the figures but not their sign, which is how it got through. Corrected in `actuals.yaml`, `projection.yaml`, `sale.yaml` (each year's net cash = old − 2 × its effect; profit, the bridge's cash-flow row, CAGR and IRR recomputed from the corrected flows by a script independent of the implementation, which first reproduced every old figure), the loader and §5.3/§10.3 (baseline 2027 −6,197.28 → −3,160.32; scenario A's profit −59,853.57 → −28,537.89).

## 13. Questions for Luis

Plain language; each with what it means, why it matters, and the recommended answer.

1. **Drop the "Nebenkostenabrechnung" from this phase?** A Betriebskostenabrechnung is the yearly statement a landlord sends when tenants pay running costs as a *prepayment* (Vorauszahlung) that is settled against the real costs. Your leases charge a *flat rate* (Pauschale) or an all-in rent, which by contract is never settled, so the app would have nothing to generate, and the phase's current done criterion ("one settlement generated from real 2026 data") can never be met. **Recommendation:** take the generator out of phase 4 and keep it as an optional step to build only if you ever sign a prepayment lease (D91). Both you and your wife would each enter one yearly figure; when the tax assessment (Einkommensteuerbescheid) for a year arrives, you type its "zu versteuerndes Einkommen" and the estimate is replaced for that year. **Recommendation:** enter both gross figures for 2026 now (rough is fine; the screen says "Schätzung"), set church tax yes/no per person and Bayern as the state.
