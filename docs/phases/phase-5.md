# Phase 5 spec: Automation — FinTS sync of the rental account, email-in, deadline reminders, rule set 2027

Nov 10, 2026 · **Status: draft by the architect, not reviewed, nothing built.** Every claim about python-fints, the Deutsche Kreditwirtschaft registration, Sparkasse's TAN rhythm, Scaleway's email service and the 2027 tax figures was checked on 10 Nov 2026 against the sources in §3; what could not be fetched is listed there as secondary and again in §16. **No household fact appears in this document**: bank codes, FinTS URLs, login names, addresses and every amount below are invented, and the real values go only into Secret Manager, the container's environment and the app's own records.

Phase 5 adds no tax law and no new model job. Its four pieces are plumbing around what phases 1–4b built: (1) the rental account's statements arrive by **FinTS** (the German banks' own online-banking protocol; python-fints speaks it) into exactly the import path phase 2 built for uploaded camt files, so dedup and reconciliation are unchanged; (2) a **forwarding address** feeds forwarded documents into the same vault and pipeline as an upload; (3) **deadline reminders** are computed on read from what the app already knows (the Grundsteuerbescheid, the loan, the checklist, the tax return), shown in Zu prüfen and on the Übersicht, and optionally sent by email; (4) the **2027 rule data** (Anlage V rule set, tariff, Anlage N rules, questionnaire) is written as a data PR once the figures are enacted, and this spec records which figures are enacted today and which are still a bill.

## 1. Scope

**In**

- **FinTS read-only sync** of the one rental account at a Sparkasse: a `bank_connection` per account (BLZ, FinTS URL, login name, the stored client state), a sync started from the account page or by a daily schedule, the pushTAN (decoupled) approval handled by polling, the booked camt documents the bank returns stored as one immutable ZIP (document source `bank_sync`, type `statement`) and imported through the unchanged `import_statement` (phase 2 §7.2): same line hash, same dedup, same balance reconciliation, same `match_import` (§4). No write segment is ever reachable (non-goal: no write access to an account).
- **Scheduled sync** inside the bank's 180-day strong-authentication window, with an in-app card when the bank demands a new approval (§4.6); gated on the step 1 finding that Sparkasse serves reads TAN-free with a stored system id (python-fints issue #174 says yes once the system id is kept, §3).
- **Email-in**: one forwarding address in a mailbox the household controls at an EU provider, polled over IMAP every ten minutes by the job worker; a mail is accepted only from a member's address, with the provider's own authentication result, to the exact token address; attachments only, each becoming an `upload_document(source=email)`, a camt or CSV statement among them imported to the account its IBAN names; the body and the sender are never stored (§5).
- **Reminders** computed on read from a catalogue in `rules/reminders.py` (as the document checklist is), shown as Zu prüfen cards and on the Übersicht, acknowledged through an append-only `reminder_ack`, and optionally mailed once per lead time through Scaleway Transactional Email with labels and dates only (§6).
- **Rule data 2027** for every per-year file, as a data PR when the Einkommensteuerreformgesetz 2027 and the Sozialversicherungs-Rechengrößen 2027 are in the Bundesgesetzblatt (§7); until then the registries' latest-year fallbacks serve 2027 for projections, as phase 4 D81 and phase 4b D99 intended.

**Out**

- FinTS for any other account or bank (phase 6 adds the household's private accounts and will reuse `services/bank/fints.py`; the depot and crypto never go through FinTS); MT940 (`get_transactions` and its `mt-940` parser are not called: the app parses camt only, D33); PDF statements by FinTS (none exist); the aggregator (SaaS only); a TAN typed by hand (chipTAN, smsTAN): the sync supports the decoupled pushTAN only, since that is what the account uses, and refuses any other mechanism with `bank.tan_not_decoupled` (§4.4).
- Reading an email's body, inline text or links; replying to mail; a per-sender rule; any mail provider's inbound webhook (§5.1 says why).
- Web push notifications (§6.4: deferred, with what it would take), SMS, calendar feeds (an `.ics` export is a cheap later addition, noted in Q-O41).
- Reminders for things the app does not model: Einkommensteuer-Vorauszahlungen (phase 4b D95), the Kaution's return, insurance renewals, the Anlage SO deadline of phases 6–8.
- Document retention reminders: the auto-delete job of the retention policy is not built, so a "may be deleted on …" line would ask for an action nobody performs; it joins the retention job when that is built.
- Any 2027 figure before its enactment (§7): the files are written from the Bundesgesetzblatt, never from a bill.

## 2. Decisions taken in this spec

Numbering continues from phase 4b's D114. Each one is a place to push back in review.

| # | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D115 | **A FinTS sync is a `BankSource` that produces the same camt ZIP the online-banking export does, and nothing else is new on the import path.** `services/bank/fints.py` calls `get_transactions_xml(account, start, end)` (segment HKCAZ; the camt formats are negotiated from the bank's HICAZS parameters, python-fints 5.0.1 `client.py`) and keeps only the **booked** streams; `rules/bank_sync.build_archive(streams)` writes them into a deterministic ZIP (members `camt-0001.xml` … in the order received, fixed timestamps, no compression, so the same content gives the same bytes and the vault's dedup on bytes makes a no-change sync a no-op upload too). The ZIP is stored with `upload_document(source=bank_sync)` as the immutable original (principle 6) and handed to `import_statement` unchanged: `detect_format` sees `PK\x03\x04` → `camt`, D34's member rules and per-report checks apply, `line_hash` is the same function over the same `AcctSvcrRef`, `reconcile` sums the stored window. A sync therefore cannot book a line an upload would not, and the review queue sees a sync's problems as the same `import_unreconciled` card. | Phase 2 input point 2 and D22: dedup on the bank's own reference; the "Bank sync (FinTS)" arrow in the ARCHITECTURE pipeline lands on the same store-and-import stage as an upload. One parser, one hash, one reconciliation. |
| D116 | **The overlap guarantee rests on the reference, and reconciliation is the backstop.** A line the export already stored is skipped by the sync when its `AcctSvcrRef` is the same in both (the hash is `ref`-based, `rules/statements._reference`), and step 1 verifies this on the real account: one month uploaded from the export, then synced, must give `lines_new = 0`. If the bank ever sends a different reference over FinTS than in the export, both copies are stored, `computed_closing` overshoots and the import is `unreconciled` (`statement.unreconciled`), so nothing reaches matching; the Zu prüfen card names the origin ("Der Bankabruf passt nicht zum Kontostand" instead of the PDF wording) and offers the cross-format duplicate review. **No `origin` dimension is added to `likely_duplicates`** (both are format `camt`); if step 1 shows differing references, that becomes a spec amendment with its own fixture (Q-O36). | The hash is the invariant phase 2 reviewed; widening the duplicate rule on a hypothesis would add review noise for a case that, per the camt standard and Luis's export (D33: `AcctSvcrRef` distinct per entry, bank-assigned), should not occur. |
| D117 | **The declared closing balance of a sync comes from the file when the camt carries `Bal`, else from HKSAL.** `get_balance(account)` (segment HKSAL, the booked balance) is read in the same dialog after the transactions; when the ZIP's last report states `CLBD`, D34 applies unchanged (`balance_source = file`); when the bank's HKCAZ documents carry no balances (possible: FinTS may deliver one camt.052 or camt.053 for the whole range, Q-O34), the sync declares HKSAL's booked balance as `declared_closing` with the new `balance_source = bank`, and `reconcile` runs as for a typed balance. Pending (`PDNG`) streams are discarded, so HKSAL's booked figure is the right anchor. Migration 0019 widens the CHECK; `DeclareBalanceBody` is untouched. | D21: reconciliation is the guard; a sync without a declared balance would sit at `pending_balance` and ask Luis to type what the bank just told the app. |
| D118 | **The TAN flow is decoupled-only and polled by the job.** python-fints returns a `NeedTANResponse` (response code 3955 marks `decoupled`) from `init_tan_response` at login or from a read; `send_tan(response, "")` sends HKTAN process `S`, and code 3956 means "not confirmed yet" and returns another `NeedTANResponse`; the library has no polling loop (§3). The `bank_sync` job therefore: opens the dialog; on a TAN demand writes status `needs_approval` (event `bank.sync_approval_needed`) so the account page and Zu prüfen show "Jetzt in der S-pushTAN-App freigeben"; polls `send_tan` every 3 s for at most 5 minutes (`SYNC_APPROVAL_TIMEOUT_S`, a setting); on confirmation continues; on timeout ends the dialog and fails with `bank.approval_timeout`. A mechanism that is not decoupled (`get_tan_mechanisms()` entry without the decoupled flag; chipTAN, smsTAN) fails the sync with `bank.tan_not_decoupled` before any read. The sync row is a restartable state machine (`rules/bank_sync.next_state`, fixture `tan_flow.yaml`): a worker stopped mid-wait (redeploy; `shutdown_grace_s` is 20 s) leaves the row `needs_approval`, the next attempt opens a fresh dialog. Two syncs of one account never run at once (Procrastinate lock `account:{id}`, and the service refuses a second start with `bank.sync_running`). `pause_dialog`/`resume_dialog` are not used: the API and the worker share one process (phase 1 step 3 §3.7), so the dialog lives in the job's thread for the minutes it takes. | A sync is a job, not a request ("Requests never wait on a model" holds for the bank too); the approval is a few taps on the phone. The user never types a TAN into the app, so no TAN crosses the API. |
| D119 | **The PIN and the product id are container secrets from Secret Manager, never in the database, a job payload, an event or a log; the client state is stored, MAC'd, without private data.** `HAUSBUCH_FINTS_PIN` and `HAUSBUCH_FINTS_PRODUCT_ID` are read by `Settings` (`repr=False`), kept in Secret Manager and pasted into the API container's secret variables exactly as `HAUSBUCH_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is (containers cannot read Secret Manager, runbook finding); the sync job reads them from `Settings`, never from its arguments (`procrastinate_jobs.args` is a database column, so **no secret ever rides in a payload**). A "type the PIN at each sync" mode is not built: the typed PIN could reach the worker only through the queue or an in-process side channel, and the daily schedule needs the PIN without a person. `services/bank/fints.py` wraps `FinTS3PinTanClient` in a **read-only facade** exposing `accounts()`, `balance()`, `transactions_xml()` and the TAN methods only; a unit test asserts that no attribute whose name starts with `sepa_`, `transfer`, `debit` or `standing` is reachable through it. The `fints` logger is pinned to `WARNING` in `api/logging.py` (python-fints at DEBUG logs whole messages, PIN included) and a test asserts the level. The client state from `deconstruct(including_private=False)` (system id, bank and user parameter data, no account names, no PIN; python-fints client docs) is stored in `bank_connection.client_data` with an HMAC-SHA256 under a key derived from `HAUSBUCH_SESSION_KEY` with the label `hausbuch-fints-state-v1`; a MAC mismatch discards the blob (`bank.state_rejected`) and the next sync needs a fresh approval. Rotating the session key therefore costs one pushTAN approval, which `deploy/scaleway.md` says. The bank's URL, BLZ and the login name are columns of `bank_connection`; the login name is masked in every event (`bank.connection_created` carries `login_masked`: first two and last two characters) and never logged. | ARCHITECTURE, Security: "FinTS PIN and any bank tokens in a secrets manager, never in the DB"; the docs' own advice to MAC the blob; no new key (the fourth would have to be rotated and documented too) but no raw reuse either. |
| D120 | **Scheduled sync: daily at 06:30 UTC, only when `auto_sync` is on and the PIN is configured; a TAN demand during a scheduled run becomes a card, never a wait.** A periodic Procrastinate task `bank_sync_scheduled` (cron `30 6 * * *`, `periodic_id` per task as `scrub_proposals`) queues one `bank_sync` per connection with `auto_sync = true`, trigger `scheduled`. The request window (`rules/bank_sync.request_window`, fixture `sync_window.yaml`) is `[last_booking_on − 14 days, today]` after a successful sync (the overlap is free thanks to D116) and `[opening_balance_on, today]` for the first; a window longer than 90 days is flagged `may_need_approval` (the SCA exemption covers the last 90 days of history, §3). A scheduled run that meets a TAN demand **ends the dialog at once** (no one is watching the phone), sets status `needs_approval` with trigger `scheduled`, and the Zu prüfen card `bank_approval_needed` says "Der automatische Abruf braucht eine Freigabe: jetzt abrufen und in der S-pushTAN-App bestätigen"; a manual sync from the card renews the window (Sparkasse: "alle 180 Tage eine TAN", §3) and the schedule goes on. Procrastinate skips a schedule missed by more than ten minutes; the container runs at min-scale 1, so the daily run is lost only during a redeploy at 06:30 and happens the next day. | Phase 2 D41's sweeper is the pattern; a daily pull keeps the inbox current without a person, and the approval every half year is one tap. Gated on step 1's finding Q-O35. |
| D121 | **Email-in is an IMAP mailbox the household controls at an EU provider, polled by the worker; no inbound webhook, no new processor.** Scaleway's Transactional Email sends only (its webhooks report delivery events, §3); Mailgun's EU region does receive, but Mailgun is a US controller that would see every forwarded tenant document before the `ai_tenant_documents` gate could act (phase 2 §11), so it is rejected. The mailbox (a dedicated address at the household's EU mail provider, or a German provider such as mailbox.org at about €30 a year with an own domain, §3) holds credentials as container secrets `HAUSBUCH_INBOX_IMAP_HOST`, `HAUSBUCH_INBOX_IMAP_USER`, `HAUSBUCH_INBOX_IMAP_PASSWORD` and the exact token address `HAUSBUCH_INBOX_ADDRESS` (e.g. `belege+k7f3q2@example.org`; plus-addressing where the provider supports it, otherwise an alias). A periodic task `poll_inbox` (every 10 minutes, `*/10 * * * *`) lists unseen messages and applies the pure rule `rules/inbound_mail.decide(facts, policy)` (fixture `inbound_mail.yaml`): **accept** only when (a) the `From` address equals a workspace member's `app_user.email` (case-folded), (b) the provider's `Authentication-Results` header whose `authserv-id` is the provider's own host (configured as `HAUSBUCH_INBOX_AUTHSERV_ID`; any other authserv-id is forgeable and ignored) reports `dkim=pass` for the sender's domain or `spf=pass`, and (c) one recipient equals the token address. Everything else is **rejected** with a code (`email.sender_not_allowed`, `email.auth_failed`, `email.recipient_mismatch`) and moved to the folder `Abgelehnt` unopened: its parts are never read. Accepted: the MIME parts with a filename and a media type in `upload_media_types` (PDF, images, camt ZIP, CSV, XML; `message/rfc822` is already accepted since phase 1), **plus the attachments of one nested `message/rfc822` part** (a mail forwarded as an attachment; one level); at most 20 attachments and 25 MiB each, else `email.too_large`. Each attachment becomes `upload_document(source=email, original_filename=<the part's filename>)`, so the classifier, the extraction and the `ai_tenant_documents` gate apply unchanged; a part that `detect_format` recognises as a statement whose `account_iban` matches exactly one non-owner account goes to `import_statement` on that account instead (a CSV then waits at `pending_balance` as an upload would). **The body, subject, `From`, `To` and headers are never stored**; `inbound_mail` keeps the SHA-256 of the `Message-ID` (dedup), the time, the decision code and the attachment count, and `inbound_mail_attachment` links the documents and imports. A processed mail is **deleted** from the mailbox (the vault holds the originals; a copy in a mailbox would be personal data the retention rules do not govern); a mail that failed (`email.failed`, e.g. a storage error) stays unseen for the next poll, at most 3 attempts, then `Fehler` folder and a card. A sender's address appears in no log, event or row. | The documents are the same originals an upload brings, so the vault's dedup, typing and retention apply; the one new party is the mailbox provider the household already trusts with its mail. The gate for tenant data stays exactly where phase 2 put it: in the job, after classification. |
| D122 | **Reminders are computed on read from a catalogue, like the document checklist; only acknowledgements and sent notifications are stored.** `rules/reminders.build_reminders(facts, today) -> list[Reminder]` is pure over `ReminderFacts` (properties with their Grundsteuerbescheid presence and §23 date, loans with `fixed_until` and `disbursed_on`, tenancies with `operating_costs_terms` and `rent_due_day`, the per-year checklist items, the filing deadline from phase 4b's `filing_deadline`, the questionnaire progress, the `wage_certificate` rows, the rent allocations by service period, the accounts' last reconciled booking day and last sync, the acks). Each `Reminder` has a **stable id** `key:scope_id:due_on` (e.g. `grundsteuer.instalment:<property_id>:2027-02-15`), a `kind`, a `severity` (`action` or `info`), `due_on`, `visible_from` (= `due_on − lead_in_app`), `label_code` and `label_args` (codes and dates; German text in the frontend; never a name or an amount), and `cleared_by` (`ack`, `auto`). §6.2 lists the kinds and their dates. A `reminder_ack` row (`done`, `snoozed` with `snoozed_until`, or `muted` for the key and scope) hides a reminder; a muted key stops every future instance for that scope until an `unmuted` row. Kinds with `cleared_by = auto` vanish when their condition ends (the certificate confirmed, the questionnaire complete, the sync succeeded, the rent allocation confirmed). Zu prüfen shows every `action` reminder with `visible_from ≤ today` as a card (kind `reminder`), counted in the sidebar; the Übersicht's new **Nächste Termine** card lists the next ten reminders of both severities within 90 days (mockups 01 "deadlines" and 07 "upcoming dates"). | Principle 4 (nothing is edited; a correction is a new row) and the checklist precedent (data entries, status on read, no stored state that can rot). |
| D123 | **One extra channel: a daily digest email through Scaleway Transactional Email, off by default, labels and dates only.** The workspace switch `reminder_emails` (Einstellungen → Erinnerungen) turns on a periodic task `send_reminders` (06:00 UTC): per workspace it computes the reminders, takes every `(reminder_id, lead_days)` whose send day (`due_on − lead_days`) is on or before today and has no `notification` row yet, and sends **one digest mail per member** listing those items (German label, date, the app's link), then records one `notification` row per item, lead and recipient (`UNIQUE (workspace_id, reminder_id, lead_days, channel, recipient_user_id)`, so a day the worker was down sends the next day and nothing is sent twice). The mail carries **no amount, no name, no address, no tenant or property label** beyond the property's own label as the user typed it (the Übersicht card is where the detail is). Sender domain, SPF, DKIM and DMARC records are Luis's DNS work (`deploy/scaleway.md` step 14); the API key is the container secret `HAUSBUCH_TEM_SECRET_KEY` with `HAUSBUCH_TEM_PROJECT_ID`, `HAUSBUCH_MAIL_FROM`, and `HAUSBUCH_TEM_REGION = fr-par`. Web push is **deferred** (§6.4). | Scaleway is already the vendor and processor (EU, DPA in place); sending is the only thing needed; a reminder's job is to reach someone who is not in the app, which a card alone cannot do. |
| D124 | **The 2027 rule data is a data PR gated on enactment, and today nothing it would hold is enacted but one figure.** As of 10 Nov 2026 the Einkommensteuerreformgesetz 2027 (Drucksache 21/8235: Grundfreibetrag 12,564, Kindergeld 267, Kinderfreibetrag 3,564 per parent, Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag 1,430, §35a Handwerker credit 15 % capped at 900, the 45 % zone from 250,000 and a new 47 % zone from 280,000) had its first reading on 8 Oct 2026 and no committee report, Bundesrat vote or Bundesgesetzblatt; the Sozialversicherungs-Rechengrößen-Verordnung 2027 (BBG RV 106,200, BBG KV 76,500) awaits the Bundesrat; the Pflegeneuordnungsgesetz (PV BBG aligned to 76,500) is on the Bundesrat's agenda of 20 Nov 2026; **only the average Zusatzbeitrag 2027 of 2.9 % is published** (Bundesanzeiger 30 Oct 2026, unchanged from 2026) (§3, §7). The repo's rule ("a change to a figure here is a data PR against a newly read source") means the four 2027 files are written in step 5 from the Bundesgesetzblatt, with every key stated explicitly (phase 4b Q-O29 (c)) and a Python test `tests/rules/test_rule_data_2027.py` asserting it. The 2026 form mappings (Anlage V, Anlage N) stay `provisional` in the 2027 files until the 2027 forms are published, as D53 did; and Q-O33 asks whether the 2026 forms themselves are already published. | Principle 5 and the two precedents (D81, D99). Writing a bill's figures would make a 2027 draft reproducible against a law that may not pass in that form. |
| D125 | **Migration `0019_automation` adds six tables and two CHECK widenings; nothing is dropped or moved.** `bank_connection` and `bank_sync` are mutable state rows with events (like `statement_import`); `inbound_mail`, `inbound_mail_attachment`, `reminder_ack` and `notification` are append-only (`forbid_mutation` on UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE, like `return_input`); `statement_import.balance_source` gains `bank` (D117); `workspace.reminder_emails boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false` joins `ai_tenant_documents` (0005); `account.source` already allows `fints` and `document.source` already allows `email` and `bank_sync` (0001, 0006), so neither is touched. ## 4. FinTS sync (`services/bank/fints.py`, `services/bank_sync.py`, `jobs/bank_sync.py`, `rules/bank_sync.py`)

### 4.1 What Luis does first (the long pole)

1. **Register the product** with the Deutsche Kreditwirtschaft: the PDF form from fints.org, product name "Hausbuch", a version string, Luis as Hersteller; emailed to the address on the page; 10–15 working days (§3). The registration number is the product id. Nothing can be tested against the bank before it arrives; the spec PR and step 0 do not need it.
2. **Find the FinTS endpoint** of the household's Sparkasse (the DK's server list is sent with the registration; the Sparkassen share one URL pattern per institute) and the **Anmeldename** (the online-banking login name, not the account number). Both go into the `bank_connection` record through the account page (§11), never into the repository.
3. **Secret Manager**: `hausbuch-fints-pin` (the online-banking PIN) and `hausbuch-fints-product-id`; paste both into the API container as `HAUSBUCH_FINTS_PIN` and `HAUSBUCH_FINTS_PRODUCT_ID` (`deploy/scaleway.md` step 14, written in step 1). Rotation: a PIN change in online banking → new secret version → container variable → redeploy, as the API key.
4. **pushTAN** must be the account's active TAN method (it is, phase 2 §7.1's export was made with it).

### 4.2 Records

- `bank_connection` (one per account; `account.source` becomes `fints` on creation, D125): `bank_code` (8 digits), `fints_url` (https only), `login_name`, `tan_mechanism` (the three-digit security function chosen at the first sync, e.g. the bank's decoupled pushTAN entry; stored so later dialogs need no choice), `client_data` + `client_data_mac` (D119; null until the first successful dialog), `auto_sync` (default false), `sca_confirmed_at` (the last approval; the UI shows "Freigabe gültig bis etwa <+180 Tage>" as an estimate, never a guarantee), `last_sync_at`, `last_booking_on`.
- `bank_sync` (one per run): `trigger` (`manual`, `scheduled`), `status` ∈ {`queued`, `running`, `needs_approval`, `importing`, `done`, `nothing_new`, `failed`}, `requested_from`, `requested_to`, `import_id` (the `statement_import` the run produced, null for `nothing_new` or a failure), `lines_total`, `lines_new`, `balance_source`, `error_code`, `started_at`, `finished_at`, `created_by`.

### 4.3 The run

```
start (UI or schedule) → bank_sync row queued, job bank_sync(workspace_id, sync_id) with lock account:{account_id}
→ running: build the facade from Settings (PIN, product id) and the connection (url, BLZ, login, client_data if MAC ok)
→ open the dialog; if init_tan_response: D118 approval (needs_approval → poll) ; scheduled → abort to needs_approval
→ get_sepa_accounts(): the account whose IBAN hash equals account.iban_hmac must be present, else bank.account_not_found
→ get_transactions_xml(account, from, to): booked streams; pending discarded
→ get_balance(account) → booked balance
→ deconstruct(including_private=False) → client_data + MAC, sca_confirmed_at when an approval happened
→ importing: build_archive(streams) → upload_document(source=bank_sync, media_type=application/zip, original_filename="fints-<from>-<to>.zip")
          → import_statement(account, data) unchanged → if the file states no closing: declare HKSAL balance, balance_source=bank
→ done (lines_new, import status) | nothing_new (no booked entry in the window, or every line already known: no document is stored, the import row stays, see §15 Q5 and Q-O45) | failed (error_code)
```

Every transition is an event on entity `account` (`bank.sync_started`, `bank.sync_approval_needed`, `bank.sync_completed` with `import_id`, `lines_total`, `lines_new`, `balance_source`, `reconciled`, `bank.sync_failed` with `error_code`); payloads carry counts and codes, never an amount, a name or the login. A failed run is retried by Procrastinate only for `bank.unreachable` (network, HTTP 5xx; `RetryLater` as `document_read` does, three attempts); every other failure waits for a person. A bank error with a code starting `9` (python-fints raises a generic `Exception` for it) is caught and recorded as `bank.refused` with the four-digit code only (never the bank's message text, which may quote the login name).

### 4.4 Errors (`bank.*`)

`bank.not_configured` (no PIN or product id in the environment), `bank.tan_not_decoupled`, `bank.approval_timeout`, `bank.sync_running`, `bank.account_not_found`, `bank.state_rejected`, `bank.unreachable`, `bank.refused`, `bank.nothing_new` (an info code on the row, not a failure), `bank.unsupported_format` (the bank offers no camt format the parser reads: HICAZS lists none of `camt.052.001.02/08`, `camt.053.001.02/08`). D34's `statement.*` codes apply to the archive as to an upload.

### 4.5 Why the archive is the stored original

The bytes the bank sends are the evidence (principle 6), and the vault, retention and the checklist already treat a statement document; a sync that stores lines without a document would be the first ledger input without an original. Pending entries are not stored: they are not evidence of anything booked, and `transaction.is_pending` stays unused as phase 2 left it.

### 4.6 Schedule and the approval window

D120. The account page shows the last run, the next scheduled run, the estimated approval validity and a **Jetzt abrufen** button; a `needs_approval` row shows "In der S-pushTAN-App freigeben" with a countdown of the 5 minutes; a scheduled run's `needs_approval` card in Zu prüfen (`bank_approval_needed`) links to the same button. `bank.sync_stale` (§6.2) covers a connection that has not succeeded for 35 days.

### 4.7 Privacy and limits

The model never sees a sync: a camt ZIP is parsed by code (D33) and the lines go to matching exactly as uploaded ones (the LLM categorization step sees masked IBANs and one line at a time, phase 2 §8). The connection's UPD is stored without private data (`including_private=False`), so no account name or second account of the login is kept. One sync per account at a time; at most 4 manual starts per account per hour (`bank.rate_limited`), so a stuck phone cannot run up the bank's SCA counters.

## 5. Email-in (`services/inbound_mail.py`, `jobs/inbound_mail.py`, `rules/inbound_mail.py`)

### 5.1 Why IMAP polling

D121. The three alternatives were: Scaleway Transactional Email (no inbound, §3); an inbound-webhook provider with an EU region (Mailgun EU: works, but a US controller would hold every forwarded lease or Übergabeprotokoll before the app's tenant-data gate runs, and the webhook needs a public endpoint with signature checks on the API); Cloudflare Email Routing or a self-hosted SMTP (a US processor, or ops the household does not want). A mailbox at the provider the household already uses for its own mail adds no processor and no inbound endpoint: the worker opens an outbound IMAP-over-TLS connection every ten minutes, as it opens HTTPS to the bank and to the model.

### 5.2 Luis does

A dedicated address at an EU provider (the household's existing domain hosting, or a mailbox.org STANDARD account with the household's domain, §3): the token address `HAUSBUCH_INBOX_ADDRESS`, an IMAP app password, the provider's `authserv-id` (the host name in its `Authentication-Results` headers, read from one test mail), two folders `Abgelehnt` and `Fehler`. The four values go to Secret Manager and the container; the address is shown on Einstellungen → Posteingang-Adresse with a copy button and the sentence "Nur Anhänge werden übernommen; der Text der Mail wird nicht gespeichert".

### 5.3 The decision rule (`decide`, fixture `inbound_mail.yaml`)

Input `InboundMailFacts`: `from_address`, `recipients`, `auth_results` (list of `(authserv_id, method, result, domain)` parsed from every `Authentication-Results` header), `parts` (list of `(filename | None, media_type, size_bytes, nested_rfc822: bool)`), `message_id_present`. Policy: `allowed_senders` (the members' emails), `token_address`, `authserv_id`, `media_types`, `max_attachments = 20`, `max_bytes = 25 MiB`. Output `MailDecision`: `accept: bool`, `code`, `attachments` (the indexes to fetch, nested ones addressed as `(part, inner_part)`), `skipped` (parts of other types, counted only). The order of checks: recipient → sender → authentication → attachments present → limits; the first failure wins, and a rejected mail's parts are never fetched (the listing uses `BODYSTRUCTURE` and the headers only).

### 5.4 Processing

Per accepted attachment, in one transaction per attachment (so one bad part does not lose the others): `detect_format` on the bytes; a statement with an `account_iban` matching exactly one non-owner account → `import_statement` (a CSV waits at `pending_balance` with its usual card); a statement matching no or several accounts → a plain `upload_document(source=email)` typed `statement` with the card `email.statement_unmatched`; anything else → `upload_document(source=email)` with the usual proposal job (`enqueue_document_read`), where the classifier's tenant-data gate applies (phase 2 §5.5). `inbound_mail` records the decision; `inbound_mail_attachment` links the documents and imports. The mail is deleted after every attachment is processed; the Zu prüfen card `email_received` ("3 Anhänge aus einer weitergeleiteten Mail übernommen") lists the documents for a day, computed on read from `inbound_mail` rows younger than 24 h.

### 5.5 What is never stored or logged

The sender, the subject, the body, any header, any address; the log line per poll carries counts and codes (`inbound_mail_polled accepted=1 rejected=0 failed=0`). The `Message-ID` is hashed (SHA-256, the IBAN pattern: a stored hash cannot be reversed to the id). A mail without a `Message-ID` is rejected (`email.no_message_id`): without it a re-delivery could not be told from a new mail.
