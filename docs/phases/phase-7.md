Every claim about Tangem, mempool.space, Blockstream, Etherscan, BNB Chain's public RPC, Solana's public RPC and the free Solana RPC providers, and Helius was checked on 13 Nov 2026 against the sources in §3; what could not be fetched is marked there and listed again in §19. **No household fact appears in this document**: the owners are A and B, every address, transaction hash, quantity, amount and holding below is invented, the chains are the four Luis named and no token the household holds is named, and the real values go only into the app's own records and the container's secret environment variables. ## 1. Scope

Builds on phase 6 (`docs/phases/phase-6.md`): `account.kind = wallet` is already in the CHECK (D156, refused at creation until now), `asset_movement` already carries `network`, `tx_hash`, `fee_quantity` and `movement_hash` (D166, D175, D178), `transfer_link` exists with three kinds (D162), the `price` table and `rules/valuation.price_on` are live (D167), and the exchange withdrawals wait as open legs for the wallet side (D175).

- **Wallets as accounts** (D179): one `wallet` account per wallet (a hardware wallet with its addresses on several chains is one account; the separate Solana wallet is another), with owner shares (D157) and a `wallet_address` row per chain. Addresses are public data the app must query, so the plaintext lives on `wallet_address` only; everywhere else they are masked and HMAC'd like an IBAN, never in an event, a log, a card or a prompt.
- **Chains and assets as data** (D180): a chain registry `rules/chain/chains.yaml` (five chains at the start: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Arbitrum One, BNB Smart Chain, Solana; any other EVM network is one registry row away) and an on-chain asset identity by `(chain, contract)`, never by the ticker, which spam tokens copy.
- **One `ChainSource` per chain, and no provider the app cannot lose** (D181, D192): Bitcoin over the Esplora API (mempool.space and Blockstream are interchangeable, and the API is self-hostable), Ethereum and Arbitrum over Etherscan V2 (free tier; EVM has no standard RPC call for an address's history, so the stored originals are the safeguard), BNB Smart Chain **balance-only** over its public RPC (Luis, 13 Nov 2026: no NodeReal key; BscScan's API is deprecated and Etherscan V2 lists BSC as paid), Solana over **standard JSON-RPC** against a configurable URL (the public endpoint, Luis's Helius Developer plan, or any other provider), movements derived by code from each transaction's balance deltas; Helius's parsed descriptions are at most labels, never a decision input. Keys and URLs are container secrets, never in a trace. A daily sync job, and the provider's answer stored as the sync's original when it brings a new movement (D182), so nothing already read depends on any provider.
- **Movements by code** (D183–D186): every transaction that touches an own address becomes one `movement_group` with its movements, classified by deterministic rules and protocol decoders kept as data (`decoders.yaml`); the fee is always its own movement; a transfer between own addresses, own wallets and own exchanges is linked by its transaction hash (D175's `chain` link) or by asset, quantity and window; swaps, wraps, bridges, rewards, airdrops and spam have their rules; what no rule explains is a Zu prüfen card with a question and the answers that fit. **No model call anywhere in this phase** (D189), so no eval case and no consent switch.
- **Lots and the holding period on read** (D187): `rules/lots.py` runs FIFO per account and asset over confirmed movements and links, every lot keeping its acquisition day and cost through transfers, wraps and bridges; the countdown „steuerfrei ab“ by §23 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 EStG with the day arithmetic of §108 AO; a cost that cannot be priced is a to-do, never 0 €.
- **Reconciliation per address** (D186) and the **year-end holdings statement** (D188): Σ movements per address and asset equals the chain's balance at the sync's block, else a card and nothing valued past the last reconciled sync; the 31 Dec statement is computed on read and exported as an immutable snapshot.
- **Screens**: Konten gains the Krypto › Wallets group (mockup 08), Anlagen gains its Krypto tab with lots and „steuerfrei ab“ (mockup 09c), Zu prüfen gains the wallet cards.

**Not in this phase**: Anlage SO and KAP, the Freigrenze meter, §22 Nr. 3 rewards against €256, the lot-method election (FIFO is the only method here; the election is phase 8's rule data, design §4), the position on LP deposits (stored as `lp_*` movements, decided in phase 8), perpetual futures and bot wallets (none yet, 6 Nov 2026), Ethereum L2s beyond Arbitrum until a wallet address on one exists, NFTs (an ERC-721/1155 or Metaplex NFT that reaches an own address is recorded as `spam` unless the user promotes it; its valuation is out of scope), Lightning, and any write access to a chain (never: the Security bullet).

| D181 (amended 13 Nov 2026 by Luis's answers to §18 and by D192) | **One `ChainSource` implementation per chain family, keyed in the registry, read-only, behind the HTTP client of phase 6 step 5b; every source speaks a protocol more than one provider serves, or has a stated fallback; keys and URLs are container secrets and never in a logged URL.** The protocol (`services/chain/base.py`): `ChainSource.balances(address) -> list[AssetBalance(asset_key, units, at_block)]`, `ChainSource.transactions(address, cursor) -> Page(items: list[ChainTx], next_cursor)` where `ChainTx` is the normalised transaction (`hash`, `block`, `occurred_at` UTC, `status ∈ {ok, failed}`, `fee: (asset_key, units)` paid by the own address or none, `transfers: list[RawTransfer(asset_key, from_addr, to_addr, units, index)]`, `program_calls: list[(program_or_contract, method_or_instruction)]`, `raw: the provider's record`), and `ChainSource.kind`. The UTXO accounting is D185. Rate limits are undisclosed (§3); the sync paces one request per second and stops on a 429 with `wallet.source_throttled`, resuming the next day from its cursor. (b) **EVM — `evm_etherscan`** (Etherscan API V2, one key for every chain it serves, `chainid` as a query parameter): `account.balance`, `account.txlist` (normal transactions, with `isError` and `gasUsed × gasPrice` for the fee), `account.txlistinternal`, `account.tokentx` (ERC-20), `account.tokennfttx` and `token1155tx` (recorded, treated as spam unless promoted), paged by block with `startblock` from the cursor; free tier 3 calls/s and 100,000 calls/day (§3), paced at 2.5/s. Ethereum and Arbitrum One are on the free tier as of 13 Nov 2026 (`api.etherscan.io/v2/chainlist`); **BNB Smart Chain is not** („Paid Plan required“). **Said plainly**: an EVM node has no standard JSON-RPC call that lists an address's transactions (`eth_getLogs` finds token transfers by topic but not native transfers, and only over a bounded block range), so an indexer is the only full-history source, and Etherscan stays for Ethereum and Arbitrum. The safeguard is D182 (3): every page ever read is stored, so a lost Etherscan loses only the future, which another indexer with an Etherscan-shaped API (a per-chain URL; Blockscout instances are said to mirror the `account` module, not verified today and without V2's `chainid`) or a self-hosted index can serve through the same module with another base URL, `HAUSBUCH_ETHERSCAN_URL`. That is standard RPC, no history scan, so D192 holds. (d) **Solana — `solana_rpc`, standard JSON-RPC only** (Luis, 13 Nov 2026: "if I stop using it then the app breaks"): `getSignaturesForAddress` (limit 1,000, paged backwards with `before` from the newest to the cursor, `until` the cursor's signature), `getTransaction` with `encoding = jsonParsed`, `maxSupportedTransactionVersion = 0` (without it a versioned transaction is refused) and `commitment = finalized`, `getBalance`, `getTokenAccountsByOwner` (for the SPL and Token-2022 programs), `getAccountInfo` on each registered stake account (its state and lamports; `meta.preTokenBalances`/`postTokenBalances` (each with `accountIndex`, `mint`, `owner`, `uiTokenAmount`) give the delta per mint over the token accounts the own address owns (the token accounts need no registration: `owner` names them); `meta.fee` is the fee when the own address is the fee payer (`accountKeys[0]`); the SOL delta is taken as **Σ lamport deltas over the own address and every account it owns in the transaction** (the token accounts `postTokenBalances.owner` names, the registered stake accounts), so rent moved into a new own token account or returned when one is closed nets to zero and is never a movement; a SOL outflow beside a token leg that equals a token account's rent-exempt minimum (the rent the sender pays for the recipient's new token account, read from `getMinimumBalanceForRentExemption` once per sync) is booked as `fee`, not as a transfer; `meta.err` set → `failed`. **Classified by shape** (D183 (3)): one asset leg in (no fee paid) → a transfer in; one leg out plus the fee → a transfer out; opposite legs of two assets in one transaction → `swap` (the given and the received); a leg to an own stake account → `stake`; anything else → review. **Native staking rewards are not transactions**: the inflation reward is credited at the epoch boundary without a signature, so it appears in neither `getSignaturesForAddress` nor `getTransaction`; the sync asks `getInflationReward` for each registered stake account and each epoch since the last sync and books every `amount` as a `staking_reward` dated the epoch's end (`effectiveSlot`'s block time), hashed over chain, `epoch:<n>`, the stake account's HMAC and `reward`, priced on that day; without this every registered stake account would unreconcile after its first epoch. The program ids of the transaction's instructions (`accountKeys`, `instructions[].programId` of the parsed form) are what `decoders.yaml` keys on (a known AMM lets a swap by shape auto-confirm, D186; an unknown one makes it a card the first time), so the decoders read standard RPC data too. **Helius's parsed descriptions are an optional enrichment**: when `HAUSBUCH_HELIUS_KEY` is set, `solana_helius_labels` fetches the Parsed Events record of a group once, stores it beside the original and shows its description as a label on the card („Helius: Swap auf <Programm>“); it never sets a kind, an amount or a confirmation, and a missing or wrong label changes nothing. `getTransactionsForAddress` (Helius-only) is not used. **History and limits** (§3): the public endpoint allows about 100 requests per 10 s per IP and 40 per method, is "not intended for production", and whether it serves an address's full history back to genesis (the Foundation's BigTable store) could not be verified today (Q-O93); several providers' free tiers serve the standard methods with archival depth (Alchemy 30 M CU and 25 RPS a month per a 2026 comparison, Helius 1 M credits and 10 RPS with the archival methods on every plan; secondary sources, §3), so the URL can move to any of them. Pacing: `HAUSBUCH_SOLANA_RPC_RPS`, default **4** (the public endpoint's cap is 40 per 10 s *per method*, and a first sync is almost all `getTransaction`), set to 40 on the Developer plan. (e) **Secrets and settings**: `HAUSBUCH_ETHERSCAN_KEY` and `HAUSBUCH_SOLANA_RPC_URL` (secrets, through `Settings` as `_secret(...)` with `repr=False` exactly as `coingecko_key`), `HAUSBUCH_HELIUS_KEY` (optional, labels only), and the plain settings `HAUSBUCH_ESPLORA_URL`, `HAUSBUCH_ETHERSCAN_URL`, `HAUSBUCH_BSC_RPC_URL`, `HAUSBUCH_SOLANA_RPC_RPS`; a chain whose key is unset shows `wallet.key_missing` on its addresses with the runbook step (§17) and syncs nothing; the Bitcoin and BSC sources need none, and Solana works without any (the public URL). The sync's trace logs each request's host and path **with the query string removed** (Helius's key is a query parameter), and the `WORKSPACE_WALLET_SYNCED` event carries counts and the host only. (f) **Terms**: own use, one daily sync per address, the data stored for the household only; mempool.space's terms for automated access could not be read (§3, Q-O86); for SaaS the Stack row already says the providers must be contracted or replaced. | Design §3 (providers behind `ChainSource`); phase 6 D174's adapter pattern and 5b's HTTP client; principle 6 (the chain is the original, D182 keeps what was read); D192 (a provider is replaceable), which is why Solana speaks the standard RPC and Helius is a label; the BSC choice is Luis's (13 Nov 2026) and matches the 6 Nov 2026 design. |
| D182 (amended 13 Nov 2026 by D192) | **A sync is a job per address with a cursor, and the provider's answer is stored as the sync's original when it brings a new movement, so the history already read never depends on the provider again; a chain movement is hashed by what identifies it on the chain.** (1) **`wallet_sync(account_id, address_id, started_at, finished_at, status, source_host, cursor_before, cursor_after, block, txs_seen, movements_new, balances, reconciled, error_code, document_id)`** (`source_host` the provider's host name, never its key or path, so every run says which provider served it), one row per address per run, append-only; `cursor` is the chain's own (Bitcoin: the last seen txid and block; EVM: the last block; Solana: the last signature); a sync reads from the cursor to `tip − confirmations_final` (4) and dedups by hash (2). Task `sync_wallets`, daily at **06:45 UTC** (before `fetch_prices` at 07:00, so a coin seen for the first time gets its price the same morning), plus „Jetzt abrufen“ on the wallet page; one address at a time, the sources paced by their limits; a failed address fails its own row only, and a provider that refuses (a 401 or 403 after a cancelled plan) shows `wallet.source_refused` on the wallet page with the runbook step for changing the URL or key, the history untouched. (2) **`movement_hash`** for a chain movement = SHA-256 over `chain`, `tx_hash`, `address_hmac` (the own address the movement touches), `index` (Bitcoin: the vout or vin index, or `net` for the one net movement of D185; EVM: the log index, `internal:<trace>` or `fee`; Solana: the instruction index or `fee`) and the `role` (`in`, `out`, `fee`), with `"\x1f"` as the separator like `rules/exchange.movement_hash`; it is unique per account (0025's index), so a transaction that touches two own addresses of one wallet (a Bitcoin spend with its change, a Solana swap through two own token accounts) yields distinct hashes. Fixture `movement_hash.yaml`. (3) **The original**: when a run brings at least one new movement, the provider's records of the run (the raw JSON pages, concatenated as an NDJSON file) are stored content-addressed as a `document(subtype = chain_sync)` on the account, never shown, never classified, never sent to the model (it holds every address in clear), and the run's `movement_import(format = chain_sync, document_id)` row counts its rows as the exchange imports do; a run that brings nothing stores nothing (phase 5 D117's rule). ## 4. Wallets and addresses (`services/wallets.py`, `rules/chain/address.py`), cases in `chains.yaml`

A wallet is created on Konten › „Konto anlegen“ with Kontoart „Wallet“ (provider, owner shares with the reason, no IBAN), then its addresses on the wallet page: „Adresse hinzufügen“ (chain from the registry, the address pasted). The service validates the format (`rules/chain/address.validate(chain, address)` → the normalised form or `wallet.address_invalid` with the chain's expected shape in plain words), masks and hashes it, refuses one registered on another own account (`wallet.address_taken`), writes the row and the event `wallet.address_added` (masked form, chain, label), and enqueues the first sync (D182). Retiring an address sets `active = false` (an event; its movements stay). A hardware wallet's EVM address is added once per chain the user wants read: the form offers „Auch auf Arbitrum und BNB Smart Chain lesen“ when an Ethereum address is added, which creates the three rows with one click, each syncing on its own.

The wallet page lists the addresses (masked, chain, label, kind, last sync with its block and „stimmt mit der Chain überein“ or the card), the balances per asset from the last reconciled sync, the hidden tokens (D187), the open cards, and „Jetzt abrufen“. Nothing on it prints an address in full; „kopieren“ copies the full address from the row to the clipboard client-side (a `GET /wallets/<id>/addresses/<aid>/plain` that is never cached and writes an event `wallet.address_revealed`), so a user can verify it against the hardware wallet without the app ever displaying it in a list.

## 5. Chain sources and the sync (`services/chain/`, `jobs/tasks.sync_wallets`), fixtures `chains.yaml`, `evm_tx.yaml`, `solana_tx.yaml`, `btc_utxo.yaml`

`services/chain/base.py` (the protocol of D181), `bitcoin_esplora.py`, `evm_etherscan.py`, `evm_rpc_balance.py`, `solana_rpc.py` and the optional `solana_helius_labels.py`, each a small module that turns the provider's JSON into `ChainTx` rows (or, for Solana, into the per-asset deltas of D181 (d)) and knows nothing of movements; every module takes its base URL from `Settings`, so a provider is swapped by one variable (D192); `services/chain/sync.py` runs one address: cursor → pages → `rules/chain/normalise` → `rules/chain/classify` (D183) → groups and movements written `proposed` or `confirmed` by D186's routing → links by `rules/chain/match_transfer` (D184) → balances → reconciliation (D186) → the `wallet_sync` row and, when new movements came, the stored original (D182). The fixtures hold the provider records as recorded JSON (invented addresses and hashes in the real shapes) and the `ChainTx` each source must produce from them, so a provider's format change fails a test before it corrupts a ledger; `chains.yaml` replays the same records through two base URLs (D192). Rate pacing per source is a `sleep` injected as in `CoinGeckoDaily(sleep=)`.

The task `sync_wallets` (periodic, 06:45 UTC, `periodic_id = "sync_wallets"`) syncs every active address of every wallet whose source is configured, oldest sync first, within a budget per run (Solana: `HAUSBUCH_SOLANA_RPC_RPS` × 600 requests, so about 2,400 on the public endpoint's per-method cap and 24,000 on the Developer plan, each `getTransaction` one request; Etherscan: 2,000 calls; Esplora: 600 requests; BSC: one balance call per registry token) and leaves the rest for the next day with `wallet.sync_deferred` on the wallet page, never a card. The first sync of a wallet with years of history may take days on the public endpoint and hours on a paid one; the page shows „Verlauf wird geladen: bis <date>“. The Solana history is read **in full, back to the address's first signature**, and classified in full (Luis, 13 Nov 2026), so the lots at 1 Jan 2026 rest on every earlier movement; if the configured URL cannot serve the oldest signatures (Q-O93), the sync records the oldest slot it reached in `wallet_sync` and the wallet page offers „Anderen RPC-Anbieter eintragen“ with the runbook step, and as the last resort the typed opening statement of §18 (5).

## 6. Movements and classification (`rules/chain/classify.py`, `rules/chain/decoders.yaml`), fixtures `decoders.yaml`, `swap_group.yaml`, `spam.yaml`

Worked examples the fixtures replay (owners A and B; every value invented):

1. **A plain receive on Solana** of 2.5 SOL to A's address `7Qx…mN4p` in a transaction A did not sign, from an address that is no own account and matches no exchange withdrawal → group `transfer`, movement `in` 2.5 SOL `uncategorized`, `transfer.unmatched_leg`, card with D175's answers.
2. **An exchange withdrawal arrives**: the exchange account (phase 6) holds an `internal_transfer_out` of 0.3 ETH with `fee_quantity` 0.0005, `network` „ETH“, `tx_hash` `0xab…01` (Binance's withdraw history, D178); the Ethereum sync finds a receive of 0.2995 ETH in `0xab…01` → `exchange_transfer`, linked by hash, confirmed; the lot (bought on the exchange on 3 Feb 2026 for 500 € per the exchange's EUR leg) moves to the wallet with `acquired_on` 3 Feb 2026 and `cost_eur` 500 € × 0.2995/0.3; the 0.0005 ETH the exchange charged (`fee_quantity` on its `internal_transfer_out`, D178 (3)) is consumed from the same lot at cost on the exchange side, no gain (D188 (1)); the moved units are what arrived.
3. **A swap on Solana** through a decoder-listed AMM: A gives 1 SOL and receives 150 units of token T (registry-listed) in one signed transaction, fee 0.000005 SOL → group `swap`, movements `disposal` 1 SOL and `acquisition` 150 T, both with `eur_amount` = T's price on the day × 150 (the received asset's price first, Rn. 58; that figure is the acquisition's cost and the disposal's proceeds), `fee` 0.000005 SOL; auto-confirmed when T and SOL both have the day's price; the lot of T gets the swap's Berlin day.
4. **The same swap with T unpriced** → the group waits in review with „Kurs fehlt für T am <day>“ and „Kurs eintragen“; nothing is 0 €.
5. **A failed EVM transaction** A signed (`isError = 1`) → group `failed`, one `fee` movement from the receipt, confirmed.
6. **A spam token** credited to A's EVM address, contract unknown to the registry, A paid no fee → `spam`, confirmed, hidden, no price asked (D187 (1)); the same token later promoted with „Das ist echt“ → `airdrop`, review.
7. **A symbol lookalike**: a token named „USDC“ on a contract that is not the registry's USDC → spam, its name on the hidden list „Token 0x9f…21ab (nennt sich USDC)“.
8. **A wrap**: A sends 1 ETH to the registry's WETH contract and receives 1 WETH in the same transaction → `wrap`, two `internal_transfer_*` movements, a `wrap` link, lot moved to WETH with its date and cost, confirmed.
9. **A bridge**: 0.5 ETH out on Ethereum to the canonical Arbitrum bridge at 10:00, 0.5 ETH in on Arbitrum at 10:20 → `bridge` proposed with the pair for review the first time; confirmed → the lot moves to ETH-on-Arbitrum unchanged; „Künftig immer so“ → `chain_pair` row, later bridges over that pair confirmed by the matcher.
10. **Staking on Solana**: A delegates 10 SOL to a stake account → the card „Ist <stake account masked> Ihr eigenes Stake-Konto?“; yes → the stake account is registered as an own address of kind `stake`, the 10 SOL are an own-to-own transfer (lots unchanged); a later reward credited to it → `staking_reward` with the day's price, its own lot with its own `tax_free_from`.
11. **An unknown program call** that moved a token out and nothing in → `contract_call`, review with the template answers; „Bezahlung“ → `disposal` priced on the day, FIFO consumes the oldest lot.
12. **A Bitcoin spend** (D185): inputs 0.05 BTC from `bc1q…k7w2` (own), outputs 0.02 BTC to `bc1q…p9d3` (foreign) and 0.0299 BTC to `bc1q…z4r8` (unregistered), fee 0.0001 → card „Ist …z4r8 Ihr Wechselgeld?“; yes → the address is registered as `change`, the movement is `out` 0.02 BTC plus `fee` 0.0001, the 0.0299 is no movement; no → `out` 0.0499 BTC plus the fee.

## 7. Transfers between own accounts on a chain (`rules/chain/match_transfer.py`), fixture `chain_transfers.yaml`

The §6 examples 2, 8, 9 and 10 plus: a wallet-to-wallet transfer of 1 SOL from A's Solana wallet to the hardware wallet's Solana address (both own: linked by the hash within one sync, the lot moves between the two accounts, consumed FIFO on the sender); a withdrawal whose exchange side has no hash (BISON) matched by asset, `quantity − fee_quantity`, network and 48 hours, one candidate → linked, two candidates of the same amount on one day → `transfer.ambiguous` with both on the card; a wallet deposit of the right amount 50 hours after the exchange's withdrawal → no match (outside the window), open leg; a deposit from a third party of the same amount in the window → never linked to the withdrawal (the hash differs, and a third party's credit is never the counterpart: phase 6 step 2's review rule), open leg; a link ended by a re-booking of one leg → the lots recomputed without it.
