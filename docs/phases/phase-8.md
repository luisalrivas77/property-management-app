Luis has not yet answered the questions in §18; until he does, the decisions that depend on them are the recommendations stated there.

**Done when** (from `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, phase 8 row): a year's Anlage SO draft reproduces a hand calculation to the euro with every line opening its lots and prices, and the Anlage KAP verdict matches a hand check.

**Measured how.**

1. *SO to the euro.* For the first tax year the app covers in full, Luis (or the orchestrator from the Steuerreport's CSV, on Luis's computer, never committed) recomputes by hand every disposal inside the one-year period of one wallet and one exchange: proceeds, cost from the lots, fees, gain. The per-person totals on the SO tab equal the hand totals to the euro, and the Freigrenze verdict per person is the same. Every row of the tab opens its lots (acquisition day, cost, source movement) and the price used with its source.
2. *Leistungen.* The year's rewards per person equal Σ units × day price from the price table, checked by hand for one month of one wallet; the 256 € verdict is the same.
3. *KAP verdict.* With every Jahressteuerbescheinigung of the year confirmed, the app's verdict (must file / worth filing with the expected refund / not needed) and its expected refund or back-payment equal a hand check from the certificates' printed figures, to the euro, for each owner.
4. *Whole return.* The whole-return estimate of phase 4b with the SO income and the capital tax in it equals a hand calculation of the joint tax (the same hand calculation phase 4b's closing uses, extended by the two forms).
5. *Exports.* The all-forms field sheet, the Steuerreport and the yearly archive are produced for that year, the Steuerreport's totals equal the tab's, and a second export from unchanged data has the same input hash.
6. *Fixtures.* Every fixture of §15 passes; no case is `pending`.

The 2026 return is due on 2 Aug 2027 (31 Jul 2027 is a Saturday; phase 4b `filing_deadline`). Whether the 2026 Anlage SO comes from the app is Luis's decision Q-O99 (§18), answered 16 Nov 2026: aim for the app, with a checkpoint on 1 Jun 2027; the done criterion is measured on the first year the app covers, 2026 or 2027.

**In this phase.**

- The Anlage SO draft per tax year: private disposals of crypto assets (§23 EStG) and income from services such as staking and lending (§22 Nr. 3 EStG), per person, from confirmed movements and the lots of phase 7. Rule data per year.
- The Anlage KAP draft per owner and tax year: the figures of each institution's Jahressteuerbescheinigung, read by the model with page and quote and confirmed by a person; capital income no bank certifies; the Sparer-Pauschbetrag; the flat tax of §32d with Soli and church tax; the Günstigerprüfung; a verdict on whether filing the Anlage KAP is required, worthwhile or not needed.

- The tabs „Anlage KAP“ and „Anlage SO“ on Steuern, with every gap as a plain to-do with its button (principle 7).
- Exports: an ELSTER field sheet for each form and one for all forms of the year (Anlage V, N, KAP and SO; D226 (1)), the Steuerreport (the separate statement the SO form asks for, with settings and positions), and the pages of the yearly archive.
- The hook into the whole-return estimate and into phase 4's tax effect.
- The Jahresfragen rows for both forms.

**Settles what earlier phases left open.** Phase 6 D170 (certificate figures, Sparer-Pauschbetrag and Vorabpauschale cards, the Freistellungsauftrag record), D165 (movements from a report carry no fee or tax), Q-O96 (a balance correction in FIFO), `hh_capital_income` bank lines. Phase 7 Q-O90 (no pooling of lots by symbol), Q-O91 (NFTs), the lot-method election, whether staking is a disposal, the LP position, the transfer fee, the airdrop question, the Steuerreport printing addresses (D179, D190). Phase 4b D95 and D101 („no capital income“).

**Not in this phase.**

- Filing to ELSTER (non-goal; the ERiC note in ARCHITECTURE stands).
- The average-cost method for lots. FIFO only (D201); the election is Luis's Q-O101.
- An importer for a trading bot's log. No bot trades real money yet (design §10); perpetual futures are typed as yearly results per platform (D219) until one does.
- Hard forks. The BMF letter's rule (Rn. 67–68: no income, the old coin's cost split by market value, the old coin's acquisition day) is written down in D210 but no movement kind carries it; a fork inflow arrives as `unknown` and becomes a to-do.
- Crypto held as business assets, mining, running a validator (Rn. 33–44, 49–52). The app states on the SO tab that it assumes private asset management, and the Jahresfragen ask once.
- Anlage KAP-INV and KAP-BET (funds held at a foreign depot without German withholding, partnership shares), old fund units from before 2009, life insurance payouts, loans to related persons taxed at the personal rate (Anlage KAP 2025 lines 28–30). The Jahresfragen ask about each and show a reminder; nothing is computed.
- The reform planned for 2027 (crypto bought after 31 Dec 2026 taxed as capital income). Not law on 16 Nov 2026 (§3); D215.
- §23 loss carry-back into the previous year as a computation (a reminder only, D205), §10d waivers (SO lines 22 and 66).
- Any naming of a product to buy or a sentence telling the user to sell („Advice and budgets“, 6 Nov 2026). The tabs show measured figures and limits only (D227).

## 4. Anlage SO: from movements to the draft (`rules/tax/so.py`, fixtures `so/*.yaml`)

Pure functions over plain data; the service gathers the arguments. Nothing here calls a model, reads a clock or touches the database.

**4.1 The pipeline.**

1. `rules/lots.lots(movements, links, positions)` (phase 7) gives the lot book per account and asset, with every `LotUse` of the year. Phase 8 passes two new arguments: the year's `lp_position` (D209) and the `movement_tax_input` answers that change a lot (D210, D212). With neither, the result is phase 7's, unchanged.
2. `disposals(year, lot_uses, movements, prices, rule_set) → list[DisposalLine]`: one line per disposal movement with its parts per lot (D200), each part `taxable`, `tax_free` or `incomplete`.
3. `service_incomes(year, movements, answers, prices, rule_set) → list[ServiceIncomeLine]` (D206, D210).
4. `attribute(lines, ownership) → per-person lines` (D199).
5. `pot(year, person, crypto_parts, property_gain, other_parts, carryforward, freigrenze) → PotResult` (D204, D205): the total, the verdict (`under_freigrenze`, `taxable`, `loss`), the carry-forward used and left.
6. `draft_so(...)` assembles `AnlageSODraft`: per person the pot, the service-income block with its own verdict, the form lines (D214), warnings, to-dos, and a source list per line (movement ids, lot ids, price ids).

**4.2 A worked example (invented).** Owners A and B. Exchange account X is in B's name alone; wallet W is held 50/50. Rule set 2026.

| Day | Event | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 Jan 2026 | X: buy 2.0 COIN for 4,000.00 €, fee 20.00 € | Lot L1: 2.0 units, cost 4,020.00 €, acquired 10 Jan 2026, owner B |
| 2 Feb 2026 | X: buy 0.5 OTHER for 990.00 €, fee 10.00 € | Lot L2: cost 1,000.00 €, owner B |
| 1 Mar 2026 | X → W: 2.0 COIN, linked by the transaction hash | L1 moves to W with its day and cost; half now belongs to A, no disposal (D199 (2)) |
| 15 Jun 2026 | W: swap 1.0 COIN for 2,600 STABLE (priced 1.00 €), fee worth 5.00 € | Disposal of half of L1: proceeds 2,600.00 (value received, Rn. 58), cost 2,010.00, fee 5.00, gain 585.00, inside the year. A 292.50, B 292.50. New lot of 2,600 STABLE at 2,600.00 € |
| 5 Aug 2026 | X: sell 0.5 OTHER for 1,750.00 €, fee 10.00 € | Disposal of L2: gain 1,750.00 − 1,000.00 − 10.00 = 740.00, inside the year, all B's |
| 20 Dec 2026 | W: staking reward 0.05 COIN, day price 3,000.00 € | Income from services 150.00 €: A 75.00, B 75.00. New lot of 0.05 COIN at 150.00 € |

Result: A's §23 total is 292.50 €, below 1,000 €, tax-free. B's is 292.50 + 740.00 = 1,032.50 €, not below 1,000 €, so all of it is taxable. Income from services is 75.00 € each, below 256 €, tax-free for both. The sheet prints B's Zeilen 45–51 and 58 with proceeds 1,300.00 + 1,750.00 = 3,050.00, cost 1,005.00 + 1,000.00 = 2,005.00, Werbungskosten 2.50 + 10.00 = 12.50, gain 1,032.50; A's lines with the note „unter der Freigrenze“. The exchange table (D229) shows X in B's name: one sale, 1,750.00 €. This table is the fixture case `so/attribution.yaml: worked_example`.

**4.3 Incomplete is a state, not a number.** A person's total is `complete` only when every part has a cost and a price and every question about its movements is answered. An incomplete total is shown with what is known, labelled „unvollständig“ (never as „mindestens … €“), and with the to-dos that complete it. The sheet and the Steuerreport of a year are refused while a person's SO total is incomplete (`so.incomplete`, listing the to-dos), because a figure typed into ELSTER must be whole.

**4.4 What phase 8 reads from phase 7 and does not change.** Movement kinds and group kinds (D183), links (D184), the UTXO rule (D185), confirmation (D186), spam (D187), the lot engine and day arithmetic (D188), `holdings_export` (D190). Phase 8 adds arguments to `lots()` and readers on top; it changes no stored movement. If phase 7's closing changes a kind's meaning, D200 (1) and D206 (1) are the two lists to update.

## 5. Questions about one movement (`movement_tax_input`, fixtures `so/airdrop.yaml`, `so/corrections.yaml`)

Four kinds about one movement and one yearly answer, each a plain to-do on the SO tab and on Zu prüfen (section „Steuerfragen“), each answered on its own card with one or two buttons:

| Kind | Asked when | Card | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `airdrop_consideration` | a confirmed `airdrop` that is not spam | „Mussten Sie etwas dafür tun oder Daten angeben?“ Ja / Nein / Verlosung | D210 (1) |
| `no_market_price` | an `airdrop` with no `price_on` its day | „An diesem Tag gab es keinen Marktpreis“ or „Kurs eintragen“ | D210 (3): 0 € by confirmation, or a typed price |
| `reward_class` | the first `cashback_reward` of a payer | „Prämie (z. B. für Anmeldung)“ or „Rabatt auf einen Kauf“ | D206 (4); applies to that payer's later credits until changed |

An answer never edits a movement; it is a new row that a later row supersedes (principle 4). The to-do disappears when the newest row answers it.

## 6. Reading the Jahressteuerbescheinigung (D216; evals §15.2)

Follows phase 4b §8 step by step: classify (already done by phase 6: subtype `capital_income_tax_certificate`), extract with citations into `CapitalCertificateReading`, parse and validate in code (`extraction_parse`), scrub identifiers, build the pre-fill proposal (`rules/prefill.py`: one record of entity type `return_input`, kind `kap_certificate`), show the one pre-filled form (phase 2 D132) with every line, its page and quote, and save through the `return_input` command on „Übernehmen“.

- **Field per printed line, keyed by a stable code.** The official sample prints, beside each amount, the Anlage KAP line it belongs to in that year. The reading stores the amount under a stable code (`income`, `stock_gains`, `termingeschaeft_gains`, `ersatz_basis`, `losses_other`, `losses_stock`, `pauschbetrag_used`, `kest`, `soli`, `kist`, `foreign_tax_credited`, `foreign_tax_creditable`) and the printed line number as read, and a mismatch between the printed number and the year's form mapping is a warning on the card, not a failure.
- **A line the certificate does not print is absent, not zero.** „Höhe des nicht ausgeglichenen Verlustes“ appears only when a Verlustbescheinigung was requested; the reading leaves it `None` and the draft treats `None` as 0 while showing „nicht bescheinigt“.
- **Checks by code before the card is shown:** `kest` within 1 € of 25 % of `income − pauschbetrag_used` when no loss, no foreign tax and no church tax is printed (a warning with both figures when not; the certificate still wins); the tax year printed equals the year the card proposes; the amounts are non-negative.
- **Which account.** By code, from the document's text: the number of a known account, compared by keyed hash (D216 (6)); the model is never asked for it and the scrub removes it from every quote. Several depots at one institution (phase 6 D165) each get their own certificate, and a certificate that covers several depots of one customer is recorded on the customer's first depot with a note on the card (Q-O122).
- **Not a certificate.** The yearly „Erträgnisaufstellung“ and the depot's year-end report look alike and carry no tax-office figures; the classifier keeps them apart (an eval case), and uploading one where a certificate is asked for answers „Das ist die Erträgnisaufstellung, nicht die Steuerbescheinigung“ with the to-do unchanged.
- **Privacy.** Sent to the model under the terms of the depot's other documents (`ai_private_accounts`, both owners agreed, phase 6 D168). Stored: the year, the institution, the figures, the document link. Never: the Identifikationsnummer, a depot or account number in clear, the address.

## 7. Anlage KAP rules (`rules/tax/kap.py`, fixtures `kap/*.yaml`)

**7.1 The functions.** `certificate_parts(certificates, ownership) → per-owner bank lines` (D216 (5)); `draft_kap(owner, …)` (D198); `kap_assessment(drafts, owners, zve, tariff, rule_set) → KapAssessment` (D222) with: per owner the net income, the Pauschbetrag used, the taxable income, the flat tax, Soli, church tax, the amounts withheld; for the household the refund or back-payment under the flat tax, the Günstigerprüfung comparison, the branch chosen, the verdict and its reasons as codes.

**7.2 A worked example (invented).** Depot D is held 50/50 and has a Freistellungsauftrag of 1,000 € for the couple. Bank Z holds an account of A alone with no Freistellungsauftrag. Neither owner pays church tax. Certificates for 2026:

| | Depot D | Bank Z |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital income | 1,800.00 | 300.00 |
| Sparer-Pauschbetrag used | 1,000.00 | 0.00 |
| Kapitalertragsteuer | 200.00 | 75.00 |
| Solidaritätszuschlag | 11.00 | 4.12 |

By owner: A 900.00 + 300.00 = 1,200.00; B 900.00. The couple's Pauschbetrag is 2,000 €, half each: B uses 900.00 and passes 100.00 to A; A's taxable income is 1,200.00 − 1,100.00 = 100.00. Flat tax 25.00 €, Soli 1.37 €. Withheld: 275.00 € and 15.12 €. Filing returns 250.00 € of tax and 13.75 € of Soli. Verdict: `worthwhile`, „Die Anlage KAP lohnt sich: etwa 264 € Erstattung, weil bei Bank Z kein Freistellungsauftrag bestand.“ The Pauschbetrag card proposes for 2027: D 1,714 €, Z 286 € (2,000 € in proportion to this year's income, whole euros, the largest remainder first). This is the case `kap/verdict.yaml: worthwhile_unused_pauschbetrag`.

**7.3 Rounding.** Amounts are kept to the cent in the draft; the sheet rounds as each line's mapping says (the `rounding` key of the form mapping, as phase 3 does for Anlage V). The Soli drops fractions of a cent („Bruchteile eines Cents bleiben außer Ansatz“, §4 SolZG, read 16 Nov 2026), as the worked example does (25.00 € × 5.5 % = 1.375 € → 1.37 €). Church tax is rounded by each Land's church-tax law, not by §51a EStG, and none was read; the app drops fractions of a cent there too (**position**, Q-O118).

**7.4 Losses.** Within a year: an owner's losses offset that owner's income; then the Pauschbetrag is deducted from each owner's positive balance (D220); then a loss still left offsets the spouse's remaining income (joint assessment, §20 Abs. 6 S. 3; BMF 14 May 2025 Rn. 274, 275; D222 (1)); stock losses only stock gains. A loss the bank withheld tax against counts in the assessment only with the bank's Verlustbescheinigung (§20 Abs. 6 S. 5 with §43a Abs. 3 S. 4). Across years: a bank carries its own loss pots forward by itself, which the app sees only as a smaller income on next year's certificate; a loss the bank certified (after a Verlustbescheinigung) and the tax office then carries forward appears on the Bescheid, and the app takes it as a typed `kap_other_income` row of class `other` with a loss in the next year, proposed by a Jahresfragen row. No loss pot is stored by the app.

## 8. The Vorabpauschale forecast (`rules/tax/vorabpauschale.py`, fixture `kap/vorabpauschale.yaml`)

`vorabpauschale(year Y, positions, prices, distributions, basiszins, rates) → list[VorabLine]`, per depot and fund, per unit and then times the units held on 31 Dec of Y (D221).

**Worked example (invented).** Fund IE000INVENT01, accumulating, class equity. 100 units held all of 2025; first price of 2025: 100.00 €, last: 112.00 €; no distribution. Basiszins 2025: 2.53 %. Basisertrag per unit = 100.00 × 0.0253 × 0.7 = 1.771 €; the year's gain per unit is 12.00 €, so no cap; Vorabpauschale = 177.10 €. After 30 % Teilfreistellung 123.97 € is taxable. If the Pauschbetrag is used up: tax about 30.99 € and Soli 1.70 €. It counts as received on 2 Jan 2026, so it is part of tax year **2026** and will be inside the 2026 certificate. 40 more units bought on 10 Jun 2025: five full months before June, so seven twelfths: 40 × 1.771 × 7/12 = 41.32 €.

The card says „Schätzung“ and names what it rests on (the two prices and their source, the fund class). A fund without a class asks „Was für ein Fonds ist das?“ (Aktienfonds, Mischfonds, Immobilienfonds, anderer) with the issuer's fact sheet as the place to look; the registry file fills it where it knows the ISIN.

## 9. The whole return and the tax effect (D223; fixture `return/so_kap_in_estimate.yaml`)

- `zve_base(...)` gains the keyword `other_income: OtherIncome | None` (per owner: `so_private_sales`, `so_services`), default `None` = today's behaviour.
- `return_estimate(...)` gains the keyword `kap: KapAssessment | None` and returns three more blocks: `so` (per person the two taxable figures and verdicts), `kap` (the assessment), `household_tax` (the branch chosen and the total). Its balance line becomes: tariff tax + capital tax + Soli + church tax − wage tax withheld − capital tax withheld (4b D101's balance had only the wage side).
- The Gesamt card on Steuern shows two more rows („Kryptowerte und sonstige Einkünfte“, „Kapitalerträge“), each opening its tab, and drops the sentence that capital income is not modelled.
- The §46 filing check of D101 changes in two ways (read 16 Nov 2026, §3). (1) Its first reason's figure becomes the positive sum of **all** the couple's income not subject to wage withholding (§46 Abs. 2 Nr. 1: „die positive Summe der einkommensteuerpflichtigen Einkünfte, die nicht dem Steuerabzug vom Arbeitslohn zu unterwerfen waren“, more than 410 €): today's Anlage V result plus the taxable SO income (each owner's §23 total after the Freigrenze test and §22 Nr. 3 income after the 256 € test). SO income is not tested on its own, as the draft had it: 300 € of rental result and A's 300 € of staking rewards (above the 256 € Freigrenze of §22 Nr. 3, so taxable in full) together, 600 €, require a return. Capital income under the flat tax stays out of the sum (§2 Abs. 5b). (2) Two reasons to file whatever that sum is: capital income without withholding (§32d Abs. 3 S. 3: assessed „ungeachtet von § 46 Absatz 2“) and a church-tax Sperrvermerk with withheld Kapitalertragsteuer (§51a Abs. 2e S. 3).
- Phase 4's Planung screen is unchanged in layout; its tax effect is computed with D223 (3). Fixture cases prove that with `other_income=None` and `kap=None` every phase 4 and 4b fixture figure is unchanged.

## 18. Luis's decisions

Nine questions that are yours to decide. Each has a recommended answer and the reason, and what happens if you choose otherwise. Everything legal that could be settled from the law or the tax ministry's letters is settled above with its source and is not asked here.

- **Q-O97 How should money you put into a liquidity pool count?** When you deposit two coins into a pool and get a pool token back, the tax office has not said what that is. Reading A: it is an exchange, like selling the coins that day; a gain on coins held less than a year is taxed at the deposit, and the one-year clock starts again. Reading B: nothing is sold; your coins are parked and come back with their old purchase dates. A usually means more tax now, B less. **Recommended: A.** It is the cautious reading, the one the letter's own words support (swapping one crypto asset for another counts as a sale, Rn. 54), and the one a tax office is least likely to dispute. The app shows the euro difference between A and B for each year before you choose, so if the difference is large you can still pick B knowingly; the choice is printed in the Steuerreport. If you have no pool, this question never appears. **Answered 16 Nov 2026 (Luis): position B, nothing sold at deposit.** In his words: „It depends on how much you take out after. If your position ends up with a different distribution of coins than what you put in, when you close the position, then you can consider that a sale of one token to the other.“ That is D209's position B (a difference in units on withdrawal is a disposal of the units that did not come back and an acquisition of the extra units). He also asked whether a coin-for-coin exchange, or a sale into a stablecoin, counts before anything is sold for euros: it does; a swap of one crypto asset for another is a disposal at the market value of what is received (BMF 6 Mar 2025 Rn. 54, 58; D200 (1)), and a stablecoin is a crypto asset like any other in the letter, so selling into it is a disposal too (answered in chat the same day).
- **Q-O98 Whose gain is it when the account is in one name?** The app splits every gain by the owner shares recorded on the account: an exchange account in one owner's name counts for that owner alone, a wallet recorded as shared counts half each, and coins moved from the one to the other keep their purchase date (a transfer between spouses is not a sale). The tax office's default is that coins belong to whoever controls the key, and from 2026 the exchange reports sales under the account holder's name. **Recommended: keep the shares as recorded,** and let the app print the exchange's own figures beside the split so you see what the office will compare. This matters because each spouse has their own 1,000 € limit. If you would rather follow the names strictly, change the shared wallet's owner shares; nothing else in the app needs to change. **Answered 16 Nov 2026 (Luis): owner shares as recorded, the exchange's per-holder figures printed beside the split** (the recommendation).
- **Q-O99 Should the 2026 crypto form come from the app, or do we prepare it another way?** The 2026 return is due 2 Aug 2027. The capital-income side (Anlage KAP) does not depend on the wallet work and can be ready before your bank certificates arrive in spring. The crypto side (Anlage SO) needs every wallet read and reconciled first (phase 7), then four more steps. **Recommended: aim for the app, with a fixed check on 1 Jun 2027.** If on that day every wallet reconciles and the SO tab is complete, file from the app. If not, prepare the 2026 crypto figures with a commercial tax tool from the same exports, use the app's draft as a cross-check, and let the app take over from 2027. The alternative, deciding now to do 2026 outside the app, removes the time pressure but means paying for and learning a second tool for certain. **Answered 16 Nov 2026 (Luis): aim for the app, with the check on 1 Jun 2027** (the recommendation).
- **Q-O100 Should the app read an exchange's own tax report?** Some exchanges offer a yearly tax report with gains already computed. **Recommended: no. Keep it as a document, do not take its figures.** The app computes every gain from the individual trades so each number opens the purchases behind it; a figure copied from a third party's report could not be explained or checked, and it would not know about coins you moved to your own wallet. You can still upload the report and compare its total by eye, and the app shows its own per-exchange totals for that purpose.
- **Q-O101 First in, first out only?** When you sell part of a holding, the app assumes the oldest coins are sold first. The tax office allows this, and alternatively an average-price method, per wallet, and you may not switch back and forth. **Recommended: first in, first out only.** It is what the app already does, it is the simplest to explain, and the one-year period is counted oldest-first under either method, so the average method would only change how the purchase price is worked out. Offering the average method would mean a second calculation engine for a small difference.

- **Q-O103 Should the Steuerreport show your wallet addresses in full?** The report is the statement that goes with the crypto form if the tax office asks for it. **Recommended: hidden by default** (shortened like an IBAN), with a second button that produces the same report with full addresses and transaction ids. - **Q-O104 Staking rewards you have not collected by 31 December: should the app ask each January?** The ministry's letter wants rewards that were earned but not yet collected counted at year end. The app cannot see them for every staking service. **Recommended: yes, ask once each January, per staking position, with a one-click „keine“.** It takes seconds and closes a gap the office's letter names. If you prefer not to be asked, the app counts rewards only when they arrive and says so in the report.
- **Q-O105 Do you want the app to suggest how to split your tax-free allowance between banks?** Each person may earn 1,000 € of interest and dividends tax-free (2,000 € for a couple), and you tell each bank how much of it to apply. If the split does not match where the income arises, a bank withholds tax you only get back with the return. **Recommended: yes.** After each year the app proposes next year's split from where the income actually came in, as amounts per bank you already use. It names no product and moves no money; you would change the amounts in each bank's online banking yourself.
