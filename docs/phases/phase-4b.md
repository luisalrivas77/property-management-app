Phase 4b is small and sits on phase 4's tariff engine (`rules/tax/tariff.py`, `tariffs/2026.yaml`) and its append-only income inputs (D83, D84). Its new tax law is the employee side of the return: the Lohnsteuerbescheinigung (the employer's yearly certificate of pay and withheld tax), the Werbungskosten of an employee (commute, home office or work room, work equipment, dues, training, telecom) against the Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag, the Vorsorgeaufwendungen read from the certificate, the Progressionsvorbehalt for wage-replacement benefits, children (Kindergeld or Freibeträge, childcare), the household's §35a credit and donations. From those it drafts one Anlage N per employed spouse and estimates the whole joint return (refund or back-payment). A yearly guided questionnaire (D110) asks the same questions every year so nothing is forgotten, pre-filled from the year before. Principle 1 holds throughout: the model reads two kinds of document and proposes values with their quotes; every euro is computed by rules from confirmed rows.

## 1. Scope

**In**

- Reading each spouse's Lohnsteuerbescheinigung by AI pre-fill into a confirmed, append-only `wage_certificate` row per certificate (§5.1, §8): pay (Nr. 3), withheld Lohnsteuer, Soli and Kirchensteuer (Nr. 4–7), wage-replacement benefits paid through the employer (Nr. 15: Kurzarbeitergeld, the Zuschuss zum Mutterschaftsgeld), employer commuting benefits (Nr. 17, 18), the social-insurance shares (Nr. 22a, 23a/b, 24a–c, 25, 26, 27), employer and period.
- Reading each month's payslip (Gehaltsabrechnung) the same way into a `payslip` row (D105): the month's and the year-to-date pay, withheld tax and social-insurance shares; the rules project them to the year, so the estimate runs all year and the certificate replaces the projection in February.
- Itemised employment Werbungskosten per spouse as typed inputs with rule data per year (D99, D100, D106, D108, D112): Entfernungspauschale, Homeoffice-Tagespauschale **or** the häusliches Arbeitszimmer (Jahrespauschale or actual costs), Arbeitsmittel (GWG, the one-year life of computers, linear AfA otherwise, also for an item bought in an earlier year; subscriptions with a work share), Telekommunikation (20 % flat), Berufsverbände and Gewerkschaften, Fortbildung, sonstige (Kontoführung, Bewerbung, Fähr- und Flugkosten); compared per spouse with the Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag; the larger counts.
- Two Anlage N drafts per year (one per employed spouse), with a form mapping keyed by stable line codes, Zeile and Kennzahl per form year, provisional while the 2026 form is unpublished (D99, as phase 3 D53); every figure with its sources (§5.6).
- The Vorsorgeaufwendungen from the certificates (or the payslip projection) replacing phase 4's default deductions (§5.7, D98), and a whole-return estimate for the joint return: both Anlage N results + the Anlage V result(s) − Sonderausgaben (Vorsorge, Kirchensteuer, Spenden, Kinderbetreuung) → zvE → tax by phase 4's tariff with the Progressionsvorbehalt (§32b) and the children's Günstigerprüfung (§31) − the §35a credit → minus the tax withheld → expected refund or back-payment, and whether a return is mandatory (§46) (§5.8–5.10, D101, D102, D109, D112).
- The same facts feeding phase 4's household zvE and tax effect for that year (D98), so the Steuerwirkung on Planung and the Gesamt card agree by construction.
- **The yearly questionnaire (Jahresfragen, D110)**: versioned questions per tax year, asked per owner, household and property, pre-filled from the previous year's answers and rows, with progress shown on the Steuern screen and an open item in Zu prüfen each January.
- Privately paid rental costs and trips to a property as a phase 3 `tax_input` (D111), so the Anlage V can carry costs that never touch the rental account.
- The Steuern screen's new **Anlage N** tab with an owner switch, a **Gesamt** card and the **Jahresfragen** flow (§10); an Anlage N field sheet per owner as an optional export step (D104).

**Out** (listed on the Gesamt card as "nicht berücksichtigt" or asked as a reminder question, §10, D110)

- Außergewöhnliche Belastungen (§33): reminder only; the questionnaire shows the household's zumutbare Belastung so the user sees whether typing them into ELSTER by hand can matter. Riester (Anlage AV), Unterhalt, Altersentlastungsbetrag, Entlastungsbetrag für Alleinerziehende, Umzugskosten (D108): reminders.
- Children beyond the Günstigerprüfung, §51a and childcare costs: Ausbildungsfreibetrag, a child over 18, Kindergeld not applied for.
- Reisekosten and Verpflegungsmehraufwand for an owner's business trips: when the employer reimburses them in full nothing is deductible, so no input exists (D107); built only if a reimbursement is ever partial.
- Versorgungsbezüge (Nr. 8–10, 29–32 of the certificate; Anlage N Z11–17), steuerfreier Auslandslohn (Anlage N-AUS), Grenzgänger, doppelte Haushaltsführung.
- Einkommensteuer-Vorauszahlungen and Kapitalertragsteuer credits (KAP and SO arrive with phases 6–8); a donation carry-forward (§10b Abs. 1 S. 9).
- Private Kranken- und Pflegeversicherung and Beamte: the questionnaire asks the insurance status each year; a privately insured owner is served by the `vorsorge_override` escape hatch, not by a rule.
- Filing: ELSTER's pre-filled return already carries the employer's figures (the Lohnsteuerbescheinigung is transmitted electronically); the value here is the Werbungskosten and the whole picture, not retyping (ARCHITECTURE, Decisions "Employment income, Anlage N").

### 4.2 The questionnaire as data (`rules/tax/questionnaire/2026.yaml`, D110)

```python
class QuestionTarget(Schema):
    kind: Literal["return_input", "tax_input", "reminder"]
    input_kind: ReturnInputKind | TaxInputKind | None     # for return_input / tax_input
    reminder_de: str | None                                # for reminder: the sentence printed on the Gesamt card and the sheet
    value_match: dict[str, str] = {}                       # step 0: picks the target rows by a coded value field, e.g.
                                                           # {"item_class": "subscription"}, so two questions can share a kind
class Question(Schema):
    key: str                                               # stable across years, e.g. "owner.commute"
    section: Literal["einkommen", "werbungskosten", "haushalt", "kinder", "immobilie"]
    scope: Literal["owner", "household", "property"]
    question_de: str; why_de: str                          # plain language; what it is, why it matters, roughly what it saves
    answer_type: Literal["yes_no", "choice", "facts"]
    choices: list[Choice] | None                           # coded options (code + label_de), e.g. Steuerklasse I/III/IV/V
    targets: list[QuestionTarget]
    carry_forward: Literal["copy", "reconfirm", "none"]   # yearly kinds copy; spanning kinds reconfirm; documents none
    default_answer: Literal["yes", "no"] | None            # e.g. the account fee defaults to yes
class Questionnaire(Schema):
    tax_year: TaxYear; version: str; questions: list[Question]    # keys unique; every input_kind exists (test)
```

Questions of the 2026 file (keys; the German texts live in the file): **owner** — `owner.pay_documents` (certificate or payslips uploaded? → `wage_certificate`, `payslip`; none), `owner.tax_class` (choice I–VI, informational for the withholding explanation, Q-O18), `owner.commute` (→ `commute`; copy), `owner.home_office_days` (→ `home_office`; copy), `owner.work_room` (→ `work_room`; copy; asks the Mittelpunkt and separate-room conditions first), `owner.equipment_new` (→ `work_equipment`; none), `owner.equipment_prior` (items from earlier years still in use? → reconfirm), `owner.subscriptions` (→ `work_equipment` class subscription; copy), `owner.telecom` (→ `telecom`; copy), `owner.account_fee` (→ `other_expense` class account_fee; copy; default yes), `owner.union_association` (→ `professional_association`; copy), `owner.training` (→ `training`; none), `owner.job_move` (reminder: Umzugskosten), `owner.own_expense_trips` (reminder: Reisekosten, D107), `owner.replacement_income` (→ `replacement_income`; none; choices for the benefit), `owner.insurance_status` (choice statutory / private / civil servant → `vorsorge_override` when not statutory; copy), `owner.other_insurance` (→ `other_insurance`; copy; the explanation says it usually changes nothing for a statutory employee); **household** — `household.children` (→ `child`; reconfirm; "a child born this year?" adds a row), `household.childcare` (→ `childcare_cost`; copy), `household.services_35a` (→ `household_services`; copy), `household.donations` (→ `special_expense`; copy), `household.extraordinary` (reminder with the zumutbare Belastung shown), `household.other_not_modelled` (reminder: Riester, Unterhalt, Vorauszahlungen); **property** — `property.trips` (→ `tax_input:private_cost` travel; copy), `property.private_costs` (→ `tax_input:private_cost`; copy). 25 questions for a household of two owners and one property: 17 × 2 + 6 + 2 × 1 = 42 answer slots; the progress counts slots.

`registry.questionnaire(year)` falls back to the latest year like the tariff (D81) and flags `questionnaire_provisional` when it does. `tests/rules/test_questionnaire.py`: every file parses, keys unique, every `input_kind` is a real enum member, every `reminder` has a text, the 2026 keys are pinned (a renamed key breaks carry-forward).

## 5. Rules

Pure functions in `rules/tax/anlage_n/`, `rules/tax/return_estimate.py` and `rules/tax/questionnaire.py`, no I/O, no model; `Decimal` throughout; warnings and errors carry a code. All examples are the synthetic owners A and B (§5.6).

### 5.1 Certificate and payslip facts

`WageCertificate` (the confirmed value of a `wage_certificate` row): `period_from`, `period_to`, `employer`, `tax_class` (1–6), `tax_class_factor: bool` (Steuerklasse IV mit Faktor, §39f; a filing reason, D101 (c)), `church_tax_marked: bool`, `child_allowance_count: Decimal` (the header's Zahl der Kinderfreibeträge, 0.5 steps, informational), `gross` (Nr. 3), `income_tax` (4), `soli` (5), `church_tax` (6), `church_tax_spouse` (7), `replacement_benefits` (15), `short_time_allowance` (15a, informational), `employer_commute_tax_free` (17), `employer_commute_flat_taxed` (18), `pension_employer` (22a), `pension_employee` (23a), `pension_employee_professional` (23b), `subsidy_health` (24a), `subsidy_private_health` (24b), `subsidy_care` (24c), `health_employee` (25), `care_employee` (26), `unemployment_employee` (27). Every amount `NonNegativeMoney`, missing = 0 with the field listed under `unread` on the row (the review lets the user type it). Cross-check warnings: `anlage_n.church_mismatch` (Nr. 6 > 0 or the Kirchensteuermerkmal set while `owner.church_tax` is false, or vice versa), `anlage_n.tax_class_6`, `anlage_n.periods_overlap` (two certificates of one owner with overlapping periods), `return.children_mismatch` (the header count disagrees with the `child` rows — informational).

`Payslip` (the confirmed value of a `payslip` row, D105): `period_month` (first of the month), `employer`, `months_remaining_paid` (default `12 − month`), and for each of `gross`, `income_tax`, `soli`, `church_tax`, `pension_employee`, `health_employee`, `care_employee`, `unemployment_employee`, `tax_free_benefits`: a `month` and a `ytd` figure (`ytd` may be unread → the projection falls back to `month × 12 − gap` with low confidence and warning `anlage_n.payslip_no_ytd`). `payslip.py: projected_certificate(payslips_of_owner_and_employer, tax_year) -> WageCertificate` from the latest month: `figure = ytd + month × months_remaining_paid` for every figure, `period_from` = January (or the first payslip's month), `period_to` = December, `tax_class` from the payslip, `source: payslip`; the draft and the Vorsorge rule treat it exactly like a certificate but with `status: estimate`. Warning `anlage_n.payslip_gap` when a month's `ytd` ≠ the previous month's `ytd` + `month`.

Examples (`payslip.yaml`): `july_projection` (ytd gross 35,000.00, month 5,000.00 → **60,000.00**; LSt ytd 5,362.00 + 5 × 766.00 = **9,192.00**; RV ytd 3,255.00 + 5 × 465.00 = 5,580.00); `january_zero_remaining` (pay ends in January before a Mutterschutz: ytd 2,700.00, month 2,700.00, `months_remaining_paid` 0 → **2,700.00**); `december_no_projection`; `missing_month_warning` (June ytd + July month ≠ July ytd); `certificate_replaces_projection` (both present → the certificate's figures, `gross_source: certificate`); `two_employers_summed`.

### 5.2 Commute (`commute.py`)

`commute_lines(routes: list[CommuteFact], certificates, rule_set, tax_year) -> RuleLines` where a `CommuteFact` = `{kind: first_workplace | collection_point, workplace_label, from, to, days_per_week, absent_days, days, km, km_car, km_collective, km_other, public_transport_costs | None}` with `km_car + km_collective + km_other = km` (validator; km integer, floored by the schema from a typed decimal).

Per route: `pauschale_car = days × km_car × Σ tiers`, `pauschale_rest = days × (km_collective + km_other) × Σ tiers`. Year: `rest = min(Σ pauschale_rest, 4,500)`, `total = Σ pauschale_car + rest`; `if Σ public_transport_costs > total: total = Σ public_transport_costs` (note `public_transport_costs_exceed`); `deductible = max(0, total − Σ Nr. 17 − Σ Nr. 18)`. An owner without routes gets `total = 0` and `deductible = 0`, and Z51 still prints the Nr. 17/18 amounts. Sources: the route rows and the certificate rows.

Worked examples (`commute.yaml`): `car_25km_220_days` → 220 × 25 × 0.38 = **2,090.00**; `public_transport_capped` (60 km by train, 220 days: 5,016.00 → **4,500.00**); `ticket_costs_exceed` (same, tickets 5,200.00 → **5,200.00**); `mixed_car_and_train` (30 km, 10 by car to the station, 20 by train, 220 days → 836.00 + 1,672.00 = **2,508.00**, the cap tested on 1,672.00 only); `employer_benefit_offset` (owner B: 100 days × 25 km by car = 950.00, Nr. 17 = 400.00 → **550.00**); `no_commute_with_subsidy` (owner A: no route, Nr. 17 = 600.00 → **0.00**, Z51 = 600.00); `two_routes_in_year` (Jan–Jun 25 km 110 days, Jul–Dec 10 km 110 days → **1,463.00**); `km_floored` (25.9 km → 25); `four_routes_warning`.

### 5.7 Vorsorgeaufwendungen per owner (`vorsorge.py`)

`vorsorge(owner_certificates (real or projected), other_insurance: Money, override: VorsorgeOverride | None, rule_set, tariff) -> VorsorgeResult {pension, health_care_basis, other, deductible, source, sources}` (the tariff supplies `social_insurance.health_deductible_share`, 0.96 in 2026, so the 4 % cut is not duplicated in the Anlage N file; the caps and the pension maximum are the Anlage N file's):

- **Altersvorsorge** (§10 Abs. 1 Nr. 2, Abs. 3): `contrib = Σ (Nr. 23a + 23b + 22a + 22b)`; `capped = min(contrib, pension_max_contribution)`; `pension = max(0, capped − Σ (Nr. 22a + 22b))` (S. 5: minus the tax-free employer share; 100 %, S. 6). For an employee below the general BBG this equals the employee share, as D84 assumes.
- **Basis KV/PV** (Nr. 3): `health_care_basis = cents(Σ Nr. 25 × 0.96) + Σ Nr. 26` (the 4 % cut, since an employee's statutory contribution carries Krankengeld entitlement; the questionnaire's insurance-status question routes a privately insured owner to the override).
- **Other** (Nr. 3a): `other_raw = Σ Nr. 27 + other_insurance` (AV plus typed liability, accident, disability, term-life premiums, D112); `cap = 1,900` for an employee (Abs. 4 S. 2; 2,800 only by override); `other_deductible = max(health_care_basis, min(cap, health_care_basis + other_raw)) − health_care_basis` (Abs. 4 S. 4: the basis is always deductible; the rest fills the cap).
- `deductible = pension + health_care_basis + other_deductible`. A `vorsorge_override` row replaces the three figures.

Examples (`vorsorge.yaml`): `owner_a_60k` → 5,580.00 + (5,040.00 + 1,080.00) + 0.00 = **11,700.00** (equal to D84's default 12,966.00 − 1,230 − 36, as it should be for a full-year employee); `owner_b_32k` → 2,976.00 + (2,688.00 + 576.00) + 0.00 = **6,240.00**; `low_pay_15k_av_counts` (basis 1,530.00, AV 195.00 → other 195.00 → **3,120.00**; D84's default would miss the 195.00, which is why the certificate replaces it); `low_pay_other_insurance` (same plus liability 120.00 → other 315.00 → **3,240.00**); `other_insurance_no_effect` (owner B plus 300.00 of premiums → still **6,240.00**, the cap is filled); `above_bbg_120k` (pension **9,430.20**); `from_payslip_projection` (A's July projection → 11,700.00, `source: payslip`); `override_private`; `no_krankengeld_no_cut`.
