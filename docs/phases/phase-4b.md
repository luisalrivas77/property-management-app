# Phase 4b spec: Lohn und Einkommensteuer — both spouses' Anlage N and a whole-return estimate

Nov 9, 2026 · **Status: draft for Luis's answers (architect), not a merged spec.** Nothing is built. The orchestrator checks it, records Luis's answers to §14, opens the spec PR (step 0 of §12) and makes the ARCHITECTURE edits of §13. Every statutory figure below was checked on 9 Nov 2026 against the statute text on gesetze-im-internet.de or the official form; §3 lists each source and what stayed unverified.

Phase 4b is small and sits on phase 4's tariff engine (`rules/tax/tariff.py`, `tariffs/2026.yaml`) and its append-only income inputs (D83, D84). Its new tax law is the employee side of the return: the Lohnsteuerbescheinigung (the employer's yearly certificate of pay and withheld tax), the Werbungskosten of an employee (commute, home office, work equipment, dues, training) against the Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag, the Vorsorgeaufwendungen read from the certificate, and the Progressionsvorbehalt for wage-replacement benefits. From those it drafts one Anlage N per employed spouse and estimates the whole joint return (refund or back-payment). Principle 1 holds throughout: the model reads two kinds of document and proposes values with their quotes; every euro is computed by rules from confirmed rows.

## 1. Scope

**In**

- Reading each spouse's Lohnsteuerbescheinigung by AI pre-fill into a confirmed, append-only `wage_certificate` row per certificate (§5.1, §8): pay (Nr. 3), withheld Lohnsteuer, Soli and Kirchensteuer (Nr. 4–7), wage-replacement benefits under the Progressionsvorbehalt (Nr. 15), employer commuting benefits (Nr. 17, 18), the social-insurance shares (Nr. 22a, 23a/b, 24a–c, 25, 26, 27), employer and period.
- Optionally reading payslips (Gehaltsabrechnungen) to keep the current year's gross pay estimate current before the certificate exists (D105): the year-to-date figure projected to the year, written as phase 4's `expected_gross_pay` with the payslip as its document.
- Itemised employment Werbungskosten per spouse as typed inputs with rule data per year (D99, D100): Entfernungspauschale, Homeoffice-Tagespauschale, Arbeitsmittel (GWG, the one-year life of computers, linear AfA otherwise), Berufsverbände and Gewerkschaften, Fortbildung, sonstige (Kontoführung, Bewerbung, Fähr- und Flugkosten), Umzug; compared per spouse with the Arbeitnehmer-Pauschbetrag; the larger counts.
- Two Anlage N drafts per year (one per employed spouse), with a form mapping keyed by stable line codes, Zeile and Kennzahl per form year, provisional while the 2026 form is unpublished (D99, as phase 3 D53); every figure with its sources (§5.6).
- The Vorsorgeaufwendungen from the certificates replacing phase 4's default deductions (§5.7, D98), and a whole-return estimate for the joint return: both Anlage N results + the Anlage V result(s) − Sonderausgaben → zvE → tax by phase 4's tariff with the Progressionsvorbehalt → minus the tax withheld → expected refund or back-payment (§5.8, D101, D102).
- The confirmed certificate figures and the itemised Werbungskosten feeding phase 4's household zvE and tax effect for that year (D98), so the Steuerwirkung on Planung and the Gesamt card never disagree.
- The Steuern screen's new **Anlage N** tab with an owner switch and a **Gesamt** card (§10); an Anlage N field sheet per owner as an optional export step (D104).

**Out** (listed on the Gesamt card as "nicht berücksichtigt", §10)

- Kinder: Kindergeld, Kinderfreibeträge and the Günstigerprüfung, Entlastungsbetrag für Alleinerziehende (phase 4 Q-O2 stays open).
- Außergewöhnliche Belastungen (§33), haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen and Handwerkerleistungen (§35a), Spenden, Riester (Anlage AV), Unterhalt, Altersentlastungsbetrag.
- Versorgungsbezüge (Nr. 8–10, 29–32 of the certificate; Anlage N Z11–17), steuerfreier Auslandslohn (Anlage N-AUS), Grenzgänger, doppelte Haushaltsführung (its own Anlage), Reisekosten and Verpflegungsmehraufwand (Z65–77): typed as `other` amounts at most, never computed.
- Einkommensteuer-Vorauszahlungen and Kapitalertragsteuer credits (KAP and SO arrive with phases 6–8).
- Private Kranken- und Pflegeversicherung: the Vorsorge rule reads the statutory shares (Nr. 25, 26); a privately insured owner's contributions are a typed `vorsorge_override` (§5.7) until a later phase reads the insurer's certificate.
- Filing: ELSTER's pre-filled return already carries the employer's figures (the Lohnsteuerbescheinigung is transmitted electronically); the value here is the Werbungskosten and the whole picture, not retyping (ARCHITECTURE, Decisions "Employment income, Anlage N").

## 5. Rules

Pure functions in `rules/tax/anlage_n/` and `rules/tax/return_estimate.py`, no I/O, no model; `Decimal` throughout; warnings and errors carry a code.

### 5.1 Certificate and payslip facts

`WageCertificate` (the confirmed value of a `wage_certificate` row): `period_from`, `period_to`, `employer`, `tax_class` (1–6), `church_tax_marked: bool`, `gross` (Nr. 3), `income_tax` (4), `soli` (5), `church_tax` (6), `church_tax_spouse` (7), `replacement_benefits` (15), `short_time_allowance` (15a, informational), `employer_commute_tax_free` (17), `employer_commute_flat_taxed` (18), `pension_employer` (22a), `pension_employee` (23a), `pension_employee_professional` (23b), `subsidy_health` (24a), `subsidy_private_health` (24b), `subsidy_care` (24c), `health_employee` (25), `care_employee` (26), `unemployment_employee` (27). Every amount `NonNegativeMoney`, missing = 0 with the field listed under `unread` on the row (the review lets the user type it). Cross-check warnings the draft emits: `anlage_n.church_mismatch` (Nr. 6 > 0 or the Kirchensteuermerkmal set while `owner.church_tax` is false, or vice versa), `anlage_n.tax_class_6` (a Steuerklasse-6 certificate: its column is not drafted), `anlage_n.periods_overlap` (two certificates of one owner with overlapping periods), `anlage_n.children_on_certificate` is **not** possible (Kinderfreibeträge are not extracted, D96), so the Gesamt card always states children are not modelled.

Payslip facts stay in the extraction (D105): `period_month`, `employer`, `gross_month` (Steuer-Brutto), `gross_ytd` (the year-to-date Steuer-Brutto most payslips print as "Jahreswerte" or "kumuliert"), `income_tax_month`, `income_tax_ytd`. `rules/prefill.py` derives `expected_gross_pay.amount = gross_ytd + gross_month × (12 − month)`; when the payslip prints no year-to-date column the proposal is `gross_month × 12` at low confidence with the note saying so.

### 5.2 Commute (`commute.py`)

`commute_lines(routes: list[CommuteFact], certificates, rule_set, tax_year) -> RuleLines` where a `CommuteFact` = `{kind: first_workplace | collection_point, workplace_label, from, to, days_per_week, days, km, km_car, km_collective, km_other, public_transport_costs | None}` with `km_car + km_collective + km_other = km` (validator; km integer, floored by the schema from a typed decimal).

Per route: `pauschale_car = days × km_car × Σ tiers`, `pauschale_rest = days × (km_collective + km_other) × Σ tiers` (a tier applies to the kilometres from `from_km` up to the next tier). Year: `rest = min(Σ pauschale_rest, 4,500)`, `total = Σ pauschale_car + rest`; `if Σ public_transport_costs > total: total = Σ public_transport_costs` (with note `public_transport_costs_exceed`); `deductible = max(0, total − Σ Nr. 17 − Σ Nr. 18)`. Sources: the route rows and the certificate rows.

Worked examples (`commute.yaml`): `car_25km_220_days` → 220 × 25 × 0.38 = **2,090.00**; `public_transport_capped` (60 km by train, 220 days: 5,016.00 → **4,500.00**); `ticket_costs_exceed` (same, tickets 5,200.00 → **5,200.00**); `mixed_car_and_train` (30 km, 10 by car to the station, 20 by train, 220 days → 836.00 + 1,672.00 = **2,508.00**, the cap tested on 1,672.00 only); `employer_benefit_offset` (2,090.00, Nr. 17 = 600.00 → **1,490.00**); `two_routes_in_year` (Jan–Jun 25 km 110 days, Jul–Dec 10 km 110 days → 1,045.00 + 418.00 = **1,463.00**); `km_floored` (25.9 km → 25).

### 5.3 Home office (`home_office.py`)

`home_office_line(fact: HomeOfficeFact {days_other_workplace, days_no_other_workplace}, commute_days, rule_set, tax_year)`: `amount = min(6 × (d58 + d59), 1,260)`; warning `anlage_n.days_implausible` when `commute_days + d58 > weekdays_in_year`; warning `anlage_n.home_office_capped` when `6 × days > 1,260`. Examples (`home_office.yaml`): `days_100` → **600.00**; `days_210_at_cap` → **1,260.00**; `days_250_capped` → 1,260.00 with the warning; `overlap_warning` (commute 220 + Z58 60 = 280 > 261 → warning, amount still 360.00).

### 5.4 Work equipment (`equipment.py`)

`equipment_lines(items: list[EquipmentFact], rule_set, tax_year)` where an item = `{label, item_class, gross, net | None, bought_on, professional_share (0 < s ≤ 1), useful_life_months | None, document_id | None}`. Per item for year Y: `net = net or cents(gross ÷ 1.19)`; `base = cents(gross × share)`; if `net ≤ 800` and `Y = bought_on.year` → `base`; elif the class has `full_in_year` and `Y = bought_on.year` → `base`; else `months = useful_life_months or the class default` (an `other` item without a typed life → error `anlage_n.useful_life_missing`), `in Y = base × months_in_Y ÷ months` where the first year counts from the purchase month inclusive and the last year the remainder, half-up to the cent per year, the final year taking the rest so Σ = base. Examples (`equipment.yaml`): `laptop_1500_full_in_year` (gross 1,500.00, net 1,260.50 > 800, class computer → **1,500.00** in 2026, 0 in 2027); `monitor_gwg_70pct` (gross 950.81, net 799.00 ≤ 800, share 0.7 → **665.57**); `desk_afa_156_months` (gross 1,190.00, net 1,000.00, furniture, bought 1 Jul 2026 → 2026 **45.77** (6/156), 2027 **91.54**, …, 2039 the rest); `chair_bought_december` (one month); `other_without_life_error`.

### 5.5 Dues, training, other, relocation (`other.py`)

`dues_lines`: Σ `professional_association` amounts on Z53; `union_dues = Σ where is_union`. `training_lines`: Σ `training` on Z60 (each with `document_id`, else warning `anlage_n.receipt_missing`). `other_lines`: `other_expense` rows by class (`account_fee` defaults to 16.00 when no amount typed, `application`, `ferry_flight` → Z61, `study_room` → Z57, `other`) on Z62–64. `relocation_lines`: a `relocation` row `{moved_on, further_persons, use_flat: bool, actual_costs, reason}` → actual costs + (`person + further_persons × further_person` of the flat in force on `moved_on` when `use_flat`) on Z62–63 with warning `anlage_n.relocation_reason` (info: job-related move needed, H 9.5 LStH). Example (`anlage_n.yaml` case `relocation_flat_2026`): moved 1 Sep 2026, one further person, flat 964.00 + 643.00 = **1,607.00** plus actual 850.00 → 2,457.00.

### 5.6 The Anlage N draft (`draft.py`)

`draft_n(facts: OwnerEmploymentFacts, tax_year, rule_set, tariff) -> AnlageNDraft {owner_id, tax_year, lines: list[DraftLine], gross, wk_itemised, wk_union, wk_allowance, wk_applied, result, replacement_benefits, warnings, sources, form: stamp}`. `wk_itemised` = commute + home office + equipment + dues (all) + training + other; `wk_applied = union + max(min(1,230, gross), wk_itemised − union)` when `union_dues_beside_allowance`, else `max(min(1,230, gross), wk_itemised)`; `result = gross − wk_applied`. An owner without any `wage_certificate` and without an `expected_gross_pay` row has no draft (`anlage_n.no_pay`, the tab says "Kein Arbeitslohn erfasst"); with an `expected_gross_pay` row only, the draft is `estimate` (pay from the row, withheld tax unknown = 0 with warning `anlage_n.certificate_missing`).

Worked examples (`anlage_n.yaml`, owners A and B of phase 4's `marginal_rate.yaml`, 2026): **A**: certificate gross 60,000.00, LSt 9,200.00, Soli 0.00, KiSt 0.00, Nr. 23a 5,580.00, Nr. 22a 5,580.00, Nr. 25 5,250.00, Nr. 26 1,080.00, Nr. 27 780.00; items: commute 2,090.00 (§5.2 first case), home office 600.00, laptop 1,500.00, Kontoführung 16.00, union 240.00 → itemised 4,446.00, excl. union 4,206.00 > 1,230 → **applied 4,446.00, result 55,554.00**. **B**: certificate gross 45,000.00, LSt 5,400.00, Nr. 23a 4,185.00, Nr. 22a 4,185.00, Nr. 25 3,937.50, Nr. 26 810.00, Nr. 27 585.00; no items → applied 1,230.00, **result 43,770.00**. `pauschbetrag_wins` (items 900.00 → 1,230.00); `union_beside_allowance` (items 900.00 + union 240.00 → **1,470.00**); `allowance_capped_at_pay` (gross 800.00 → applied 800.00, result 0.00); `estimate_without_certificate` (expected_gross_pay 60,000.00 only → result 58,770.00, warning).

### 5.7 Vorsorgeaufwendungen per owner (`vorsorge.py`)

`vorsorge(owner_certificates, override: VorsorgeOverride | None, rule_set, tariff) -> VorsorgeResult {pension, health_care_basis, other, deductible, sources}` (the tariff supplies `social_insurance.health_deductible_share`, 0.96 in 2026, so the 4 % cut is not duplicated in the Anlage N file; the caps and the pension maximum are the Anlage N file's, §4):

- **Altersvorsorge** (§10 Abs. 1 Nr. 2, Abs. 3): `contrib = Σ (Nr. 23a + Nr. 23b + Nr. 22a + Nr. 22b)`; `capped = min(contrib, pension_max_contribution)` (per owner; the spouses' sum is the doubled maximum, S. 2); `pension = max(0, capped − Σ (Nr. 22a + Nr. 22b))` (S. 5: minus the tax-free employer share; 100 %, S. 6). For an employee below the general BBG this equals the employee share, as D84 assumes.
- **Basis KV/PV** (Nr. 3): `health_care_basis = cents(Σ Nr. 25 × tariff.social_insurance.health_deductible_share) + Σ Nr. 26` (the 4 % cut, 0.96, since an employee's statutory contribution carries Krankengeld entitlement; a `vorsorge_override` can set `krankengeld_entitlement = false`).
- **Other** (Nr. 3a): `other_raw = Σ Nr. 27` (AV; other insurance is not typed in 4b); `cap = 1,900` for an employee (someone with a tax-free employer subsidy, Abs. 4 S. 2; `2,800` only by override); `other_deductible = max(health_care_basis, min(cap, health_care_basis + other_raw)) − health_care_basis` (Abs. 4 S. 4: the basis is always deductible; the rest fills the cap).
- `deductible = pension + health_care_basis + other_deductible`. A `vorsorge_override` row (`{pension, health_care_basis, other}` typed, for a privately insured owner or a Beamter) replaces the three figures.

Examples (`vorsorge.yaml`): `owner_a_60k` → pension 5,580.00, basis 5,040.00 + 1,080.00 = 6,120.00, other 0.00 (the cap is used up) → **11,700.00**, equal to D84's default 12,966.00 − 1,230 − 36 (so phase 4's estimate and the certified figure agree for this owner, as they should); `owner_b_45k` → 4,185.00 + 4,590.00 → **8,775.00**; `low_pay_15k_av_counts` (gross 15,000: basis 1,260.00 + 270.00 = 1,530.00, AV 195.00 → other 195.00 → total 1,395.00 + 1,725.00 = **3,120.00**; D84's default would give 1,530.00 for the KV/PV part, a 195.00 difference, which is why the certificate replaces the default); `above_bbg_120k` (RV 9,430.20 + 9,430.20 = 18,860.40 < 30,826 → pension **9,430.20**); `override_private`.

## 14. Questions for Luis

Plain language; each says what it means, why it matters, and the recommended answer. Your answers go into step 0.

1. **How does each of you get to work?** The tax office pays a flat amount per working day for the one-way distance between home and your regular workplace (the Entfernungspauschale, 38 cents per kilometre from 2026, whatever the transport). For each of you I need: the workplace address, the one-way distance in kilometres (shortest road route), the days you actually went there in 2026, and how (own car, train or bus, bike or on foot); if you used public transport, the ticket costs for the year (if they are higher than the flat amount, the tickets count instead, so keep the receipts or the Deutschlandticket invoices). **Recommendation:** estimate the days from your calendar (a full-time year is about 220 days minus home-office days), and tell me whether an employer pays part of a ticket (that shows on the certificate as "Nr. 17" and is deducted automatically).
2. **How many days did each of you work from home, and does your employer give you a desk?** Each home-office day is worth 6 € (up to 1,260 € a year, that is 210 days). The form asks the days in two separate boxes: days you worked *mainly* at home and did not go to the office at all (box 58, the usual case when the employer does provide a desk), and days you worked at home *at all* because no other workplace is permanently available (box 59, rare: no desk at the employer). A day can only be in one box, and a day in box 58 cannot also be a commute day. **Recommendation:** count your home-office days from the calendar and use box 58 for both of you unless one of you genuinely has no desk at the employer.
3. **What did you buy for work in 2026?** Laptop, monitor, desk, chair, phone, software, work bag, professional books: each item counts, in full in 2026 if it cost up to 800 € net (about 952 € gross) or if it is a computer or software (the tax office accepts a one-year life for those); a more expensive desk or chair is spread over its useful life (13 years for furniture). If you also use it privately, only the work share counts (half is common for a phone). **Recommendation:** list every item above about 110 € with its receipt, price, purchase date and your honest work share; anything smaller goes in as "Sonstiges" without a receipt.
4. **Is either of you in a union or a professional association?** Membership fees are work costs. New from 2026: *union* fees count on top of the standard 1,230 € allowance, so they save tax even when your other work costs are below the allowance; other associations (a chamber, a professional body) count only as part of the itemised costs. **Recommendation:** enter the yearly fee and tick "Gewerkschaft" where it applies.
5. **Did either of you move house in 2026 for a job reason?** A job-related move (the usual test: it saves at least an hour a day of commuting) lets you deduct the moving costs plus a flat 964 € for the mover plus 643 € per further household member. **Recommendation:** if there was no job-related move, answer no and the Umzug input stays unused.
6. **Do you want to upload your payslips every month, or type the yearly gross once?** The app needs each of your expected gross pay for the year to estimate the tax saving on the flat (phase 4 already asks for it). Payslips carry the running total, so the app could update the estimate monthly from them; but a payslip also carries your tax ID, social-insurance number and bank account, and the yearly certificate (Lohnsteuerbescheinigung, which your employer hands out by the end of February) replaces all of them. **Recommendation:** do not upload twelve payslips a person. Type the expected gross once (you already did for phase 4), upload the December payslip if you want a check, and upload the Lohnsteuerbescheinigung 2026 for each of you when it arrives in February 2027; that is the document the return is built from.
7. **Whose documents may be sent to the AI, and is your wife fine with it?** To read a Lohnsteuerbescheinigung the app sends the whole page to Anthropic in the US, under the same contract as your purchase contract and loan contract already were (no training, deleted within 30 days). It then keeps only the tax figures, the employer's name and the period; it never stores the tax ID, date of birth, social-insurance number or bank account, and a check removes them from the quoted text if a quote happens to catch one. For your own documents that is the same decision you already took. For your wife's, she should agree. **Recommendation:** tell her what is sent and kept, and let me record her agreement with the date in this spec, as we did with the tenants' notice; no switch is needed in the app. If she would rather decide document by document, say so: then the app gets a per-person switch, and her certificate can always be typed in by hand instead (about fifteen figures, ten minutes).
8. **Did either of you receive a wage-replacement benefit in 2026?** Elterngeld, Krankengeld (sick pay from the Krankenkasse after six weeks), Arbeitslosengeld, Kurzarbeitergeld or Mutterschaftsgeld are tax-free themselves but raise the tax rate on everything else (the Progressionsvorbehalt). Kurzarbeitergeld appears on the certificate (Nr. 15) and is picked up automatically; the others are paid by an authority, so you would type the yearly amount. For 6,000 € of Elterngeld the household in the worked example pays about 785 € more. **Recommendation:** if none, nothing to do; if yes, type the amount from the authority's letter when you have it.
9. **Anything the estimate should warn you about?** The whole-return estimate deliberately ignores children (Kindergeld and child allowances), medical and other extraordinary costs, cleaning or craftsman services in your home (haushaltsnahe Dienstleistungen, Handwerker), donations, Riester contributions, and any advance tax payments the Finanzamt may have set; it says so on the screen. **Recommendation:** tell me which of these apply to you in 2026 (yes or no each), so the warning names what is actually missing and we know which way the estimate is off; adding any of them later is a small data step, not a rebuild.
10. **Is either of you privately health-insured, or a civil servant?** The app reads your health and pension contributions from the certificate and assumes statutory insurance with sick-pay entitlement (the standard case; it cuts 4 % off the health contribution as the law does). A privately insured person or a Beamter has different figures. **Recommendation:** if both of you are in a statutory Krankenkasse as employees, answer "statutory, both" and nothing else is needed; otherwise the app offers a manual Vorsorge entry.
